1. **【1987-1-3 分】** 设函数 $f,g$ 连续可微，

$$
\displaystyle u=f(x,xy),
\qquad
v=g(x+xy),
$$

求 $\displaystyle \dfrac{\partial u}{\partial x}$，$\displaystyle \dfrac{\partial v}{\partial x}$。

<details>
<summary>查看答案与解析</summary>

<div className="exercise-label-row">
<strong>答案：</strong>
<div className="exercise-label-body">

$$
\displaystyle \dfrac{\partial u}{\partial x}
=f_{1}(x,xy)+y f_{2}(x,xy),
$$

</div>
</div>

$$
\displaystyle \dfrac{\partial v}{\partial x}
=(1+y)g'(x+xy).
$$

**解析：** 对 $\displaystyle u=f(x,xy)$，令

$$
\displaystyle \xi=x,
\qquad
\eta=xy.
$$

在求关于 $x$ 的偏导数时，将 $y$ 视为常数，因此

$$
\displaystyle \xi_{x}=1,
\qquad
\eta_{x}=y.
$$

由复合函数求导法则，

$$
\displaystyle u_{x}
=f_{1}(\xi,\eta)\xi_{x}+f_{2}(\xi,\eta)\eta_{x}
=f_{1}(x,xy)+y f_{2}(x,xy).
$$

同理，令 $\displaystyle s=x+xy=x(1+y)$，则

$$
\displaystyle s_{x}=1+y,
$$

所以

$$
\displaystyle v_{x}=g'(s)s_{x}=(1+y)g'(x+xy).
$$

</details>

2. **【1989-12-5 分】** 设

$$
\displaystyle z=f(2x-y)+g(x,xy),
$$

其中函数 $f(t)$ 二阶可导，$g(u,v)$ 具有连续的二阶偏导数，求

$$
\displaystyle \dfrac{\partial^{2}z}{\partial x\partial y}.
$$

<details>
<summary>查看答案与解析</summary>

<div className="exercise-label-row">
<strong>答案：</strong>
<div className="exercise-label-body">

$$
\displaystyle \dfrac{\partial^{2}z}{\partial x\partial y}
=-2f''(2x-y)+xg_{12}(x,xy)+g_{2}(x,xy)+xyg_{22}(x,xy).
$$

</div>
</div>

**解析：** 先对 $x$ 求偏导：

$$
\displaystyle z_{x}
=2f'(2x-y)+g_{1}(x,xy)+y g_{2}(x,xy).
$$

再对 $y$ 求偏导。第一项为

$$
\displaystyle \dfrac{\partial}{\partial y}\left[2f'(2x-y)\right]
=-2f''(2x-y).
$$

对于第二项，因 $g_{1}$ 的两个自变量为 $x$ 与 $xy$，故

$$
\displaystyle \dfrac{\partial}{\partial y}g_{1}(x,xy)
=xg_{12}(x,xy).
$$

对于第三项，利用乘积法则：

$$
\begin{aligned}
\displaystyle \dfrac{\partial}{\partial y}\left[y g_{2}(x,xy)\right]
&=g_{2}(x,xy)+y\cdot xg_{22}(x,xy)\\
&=g_{2}(x,xy)+xyg_{22}(x,xy).
\end{aligned}
$$

合并即得结论。

</details>

3. **【1992-45-5 分】** 设

$$
\displaystyle z=\sin(xy)+\varphi\!\left(x,\dfrac{x}{y}\right),
$$

其中 $\varphi(u,v)$ 具有二阶偏导数，求

$$
\displaystyle \dfrac{\partial^{2}z}{\partial x\partial y}.
$$

<details>
<summary>查看答案与解析</summary>

**答案：** 令 $\displaystyle u=x$，$\displaystyle v=\dfrac{x}{y}$，则

$$
\boxed{
\displaystyle z_{xy}
=\cos(xy)-xy\sin(xy)
-\dfrac{x}{y^{2}}\varphi_{12}
-\dfrac{1}{y^{2}}\varphi_{2}
-\dfrac{x}{y^{3}}\varphi_{22}
},
$$

其中各阶偏导数均在 $\displaystyle \left(x,\dfrac{x}{y}\right)$ 处取值。

**解析：** 先对 $x$ 求偏导：

$$
\displaystyle z_{x}
=y\cos(xy)+\varphi_{1}\!\left(x,\dfrac{x}{y}\right)
+\dfrac{1}{y}\varphi_{2}\!\left(x,\dfrac{x}{y}\right).
$$

再对 $y$ 求偏导。首先

$$
\displaystyle \dfrac{\partial}{\partial y}[y\cos(xy)]
=\cos(xy)-xy\sin(xy).
$$

由于

$$
\displaystyle \dfrac{\partial}{\partial y}\left(\dfrac{x}{y}\right)
=-\dfrac{x}{y^{2}},
$$

故

$$
\displaystyle \dfrac{\partial}{\partial y}\varphi_{1}
=-\dfrac{x}{y^{2}}\varphi_{12}.
$$

又

$$
\begin{aligned}
\displaystyle \dfrac{\partial}{\partial y}\left(\dfrac{1}{y}\varphi_{2}\right)
&=-\dfrac{1}{y^{2}}\varphi_{2}
+\dfrac{1}{y}\varphi_{22}\left(-\dfrac{x}{y^{2}}\right)\\
&=-\dfrac{1}{y^{2}}\varphi_{2}-\dfrac{x}{y^{3}}\varphi_{22}.
\end{aligned}
$$

三部分相加即得答案。

</details>

<details>
<summary>查看答案与解析</summary>

<div className="exercise-label-body">

\displaystyle 0.

</div>

\displaystyle \int_{x-y}^{x+y}\psi(t)\,dt
=\Psi(x+y)-\Psi(x-y).
$$

因此

$$
\begin{aligned}
\displaystyle u(x,y)
={}&[\varphi(x+y)+\Psi(x+y)]\\
&+[\varphi(x-y)-\Psi(x-y)].
\end{aligned}
$$

记

$$
\displaystyle F(s)=\varphi(s)+\Psi(s),\qquad
G(t)=\varphi(t)-\Psi(t),
$$

则

$$
\displaystyle u(x,y)=F(x+y)+G(x-y).
$$

于是

$$
\displaystyle u_{xx}=F''(x+y)+G''(x-y),
$$

$$
\displaystyle u_{yy}=F''(x+y)+G''(x-y).
$$

故

$$
\displaystyle u_{xx}=u_{yy}.
$$

</details>
