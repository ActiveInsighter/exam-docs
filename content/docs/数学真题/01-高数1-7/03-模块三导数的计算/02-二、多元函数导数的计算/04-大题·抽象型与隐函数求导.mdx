# 大题·抽象型与隐函数求导

## 大题

### （二）抽象型

2. **【1988-12-6 分】** 设

$$
\displaystyle u=yf\left(\dfrac{x}{y}\right)+xg\left(\dfrac{y}{x}\right),
$$

其中 $f,g$ 具有二阶连续导数，求

$$
\displaystyle x\dfrac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}
+y\dfrac{\partial^2u}{\partial x\partial y}.
$$

<details>
<summary>查看答案与解析</summary>

<div className="exercise-label-row">
<strong>答案：</strong>
<div className="exercise-label-body">

$$
\boxed{\displaystyle 0}
$$

</div>
</div>

**解析：** 先观察函数的齐次性。对任意 $\lambda>0$，

$$
\begin{aligned}
\displaystyle u(\lambda x,\lambda y)
&=\lambda y f\left(\dfrac{\lambda x}{\lambda y}\right)
+\lambda x g\left(\dfrac{\lambda y}{\lambda x}\right)\\
&=\lambda\left[
 y f\left(\dfrac{x}{y}\right)
+x g\left(\dfrac{y}{x}\right)
\right]\\
&=\lambda u(x,y).
\end{aligned}
$$

因此 $u$ 是关于 $x,y$ 的一阶齐次函数。由欧拉齐次函数定理，

$$
\displaystyle xu_x+yu_y=u.
$$

对上式关于 $x$ 求偏导：

$$
\displaystyle u_x+xu_{xx}+yu_{yx}=u_x.
$$

消去两边的 $u_x$，并利用

$$
\displaystyle u_{yx}=u_{xy},
$$

得到

$$
\displaystyle xu_{xx}+yu_{xy}=0.
$$

所以所求结果为

$$
\boxed{\displaystyle 0}.
$$

若直接逐项求二阶偏导，计算较长；识别一阶齐次结构并使用欧拉定理是本题的核心。

</details>

3. **【1993-2-6 分】** 设

$$
\displaystyle z=x^3f\left(xy,\dfrac{y}{x}\right),
$$

$f$ 具有连续二阶偏导数，求

$$
\displaystyle \dfrac{\partial z}{\partial y},
\qquad
\dfrac{\partial^2z}{\partial y^2},
\qquad
\dfrac{\partial^2z}{\partial x\partial y}.
$$

<details>
<summary>查看答案与解析</summary>

**答案：** 令

$$
\displaystyle u=xy,\qquad v=\dfrac{y}{x},
$$

则

$$
\boxed{
\displaystyle z_y=x^4f_u+x^2f_v
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
\displaystyle z_{yy}=x^5f_{uu}+2x^3f_{uv}+xf_{vv}
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
\displaystyle z_{xy}
=4x^3f_u+x^4y f_{uu}+2xf_v-yf_{vv}
}
$$

其中各偏导数均在 $\displaystyle (u,v)=\left(xy,\dfrac{y}{x}\right)$ 处取值。

**解析：** 记

$$
\displaystyle u=xy,\qquad v=\dfrac{y}{x}.
$$

则

$$
\displaystyle u_y=x,\qquad v_y=\dfrac1x,
$$

所以

$$
\begin{aligned}
\displaystyle z_y
&=x^3\left(f_u u_y+f_v v_y\right)\\
&=x^3\left(xf_u+\dfrac1x f_v\right)\\
&=x^4f_u+x^2f_v.
\end{aligned}
$$

再对 $y$ 求偏导：

$$
\begin{aligned}
\displaystyle z_{yy}
&=x^4\left(f_{uu}u_y+f_{uv}v_y\right)
+x^2\left(f_{vu}u_y+f_{vv}v_y\right)\\
&=x^4\left(xf_{uu}+\dfrac1x f_{uv}\right)
+x^2\left(xf_{vu}+\dfrac1x f_{vv}\right).
\end{aligned}
$$

因 $f$ 有连续二阶偏导数，

$$
\displaystyle f_{vu}=f_{uv},
$$

故

$$
\displaystyle z_{yy}=x^5f_{uu}+2x^3f_{uv}+xf_{vv}.
$$

下面由

$$
\displaystyle z_y=x^4f_u+x^2f_v
$$

对 $x$ 求偏导。注意

$$
\displaystyle u_x=y,
\qquad
v_x=-\dfrac{y}{x^2}.
$$

于是

$$
\begin{aligned}
\displaystyle z_{xy}
={}&4x^3f_u
+x^4\left(yf_{uu}-\dfrac{y}{x^2}f_{uv}\right)\\
&+2xf_v
+x^2\left(yf_{vu}-\dfrac{y}{x^2}f_{vv}\right).
\end{aligned}
$$

利用 $\displaystyle f_{uv}=f_{vu}$，中间的混合偏导项相消，得到

$$
\displaystyle z_{xy}
=4x^3f_u+x^4y f_{uu}+2xf_v-yf_{vv}.
$$

本题中 $z_{xy}$ 的两个 $f_{uv}$ 项恰好相消，是化简时容易遗漏的关键步骤。

</details>

4. **【1996-12-6 分】** 设变换 $\displaystyle \begin{cases}u=x-2y,\\ v=x+ay\end{cases}$ 可把方程 $\displaystyle 6\dfrac{\partial^{2}z}{\partial x^{2}}+\dfrac{\partial^{2}z}{\partial x\partial y}-\dfrac{\partial^{2}z}{\partial y^{2}}=0$ 简化为 $\displaystyle \dfrac{\partial^{2}z}{\partial u\partial v}=0$，求常数 $a$。

<details>
<summary>查看答案与解析</summary>

<div className="exercise-label-row">
<strong>答案：</strong>
<div className="exercise-label-body">

$$
\boxed{\displaystyle a=3}
$$

</div>
</div>

**解析：** 由

$$
\displaystyle u=x-2y,\qquad v=x+ay,
$$

可得微分算子

$$
\displaystyle \dfrac{\partial}{\partial x}
=\dfrac{\partial}{\partial u}+\dfrac{\partial}{\partial v},
\qquad
\dfrac{\partial}{\partial y}
=-2\dfrac{\partial}{\partial u}+a\dfrac{\partial}{\partial v}.
$$

于是

$$
\displaystyle z_{xx}=z_{uu}+2z_{uv}+z_{vv},
$$

$$
\displaystyle z_{xy}=-2z_{uu}+(a-2)z_{uv}+az_{vv},
$$

$$
\displaystyle z_{yy}=4z_{uu}-4az_{uv}+a^{2}z_{vv}.
$$

代入原方程，得

$$
\begin{aligned}
\displaystyle 6z_{xx}+z_{xy}-z_{yy}
&=(10+5a)z_{uv}+(6+a-a^{2})z_{vv}.
\end{aligned}
$$

要使方程只含混合偏导数，必须有

$$
\displaystyle 6+a-a^{2}=0,
$$

即

$$
\displaystyle a=3\quad\text{或}\quad a=-2.
$$

当 $\displaystyle a=-2$ 时，$\displaystyle v=x-2y=u$，变量变换退化，不能采用。因此

$$
\boxed{\displaystyle a=3}.
$$

此时原方程化为

$$
\displaystyle 25z_{uv}=0,
$$

即 $\displaystyle z_{uv}=0$。

</details>

5. **【2001-1-6 分】** 设函数 $\displaystyle z=f(x,y)$ 在点 $(1,1)$ 处可微，且 $\displaystyle f(1,1)=1$，$\displaystyle f_x(1,1)=2$，$\displaystyle f_y(1,1)=3$，$\displaystyle \varphi(x)=f(x,f(x,x))$，求 $\displaystyle \left.\dfrac{d}{dx}\varphi^{3}(x)\right|_{x=1}$。

<details>
<summary>查看答案与解析</summary>

<div className="exercise-label-row">
<strong>答案：</strong>
<div className="exercise-label-body">

$$
\boxed{\displaystyle 51}
$$

</div>
</div>

**解析：** 令

$$
\displaystyle h(x)=f(x,x),
$$

则 $\displaystyle \varphi(x)=f(x,h(x))$。由已知条件，

$$
\displaystyle h(1)=f(1,1)=1,

$$
\displaystyle h'(1)=f_x(1,1)+f_y(1,1)=2+3=5.

于是

\begin{aligned}
\displaystyle \left.\dfrac{d}{dx}\varphi^{3}(x)\right|_{x=1}

6. **【2003-34-8 分】** 设 $\displaystyle f(u,v)$ 具有二阶连续偏导数，且满足 $\displaystyle f_{uu}+f_{vv}=1$，又 $\displaystyle g(x,y)=f\left(xy,\dfrac{1}{2}(x^{2}-y^{2})\right)$，求 $\displaystyle g_{xx}+g_{yy}$。

<details>
<summary>查看答案与解析</summary>

<div className="exercise-label-row">
<strong>答案：</strong>
<div className="exercise-label-body">

$$
\boxed{\displaystyle g_{xx}+g_{yy}=x^{2}+y^{2}}
$$

</div>
</div>

**解析：** 令

$$
\displaystyle u=xy,
\qquad
v=\dfrac{1}{2}(x^{2}-y^{2}),
$$

则

$$
\displaystyle u_x=y,\quad u_y=x,\quad u_{xx}=u_{yy}=0,
$$

$$
\displaystyle v_x=x,\quad v_y=-y,\quad v_{xx}=1,\quad v_{yy}=-1.
$$

由二阶复合函数求导公式，

$$
\displaystyle g_{xx}
=f_{uu}u_x^{2}+2f_{uv}u_xv_x+f_{vv}v_x^{2}
+f_u u_{xx}+f_v v_{xx},
$$

$$
\displaystyle g_{yy}
=f_{uu}u_y^{2}+2f_{uv}u_yv_y+f_{vv}v_y^{2}
+f_u u_{yy}+f_v v_{yy}.
$$

两式相加，得

$$
\begin{aligned}
\displaystyle g_{xx}+g_{yy}
={}&(x^{2}+y^{2})f_{uu}
+2(xy-xy)f_{uv}\\
&+(x^{2}+y^{2})f_{vv}
+(1-1)f_v\\
={}&(x^{2}+y^{2})(f_{uu}+f_{vv}).
\end{aligned}
$$

由 $\displaystyle f_{uu}+f_{vv}=1$，故

$$
\boxed{\displaystyle g_{xx}+g_{yy}=x^{2}+y^{2}}.
$$

</details>

8. **【2005-34-8 分】** 设 $\displaystyle f(u)$ 具有二阶连续导数，且 $\displaystyle g(x,y)=f\left(\dfrac{y}{x}\right)+y f\left(\dfrac{x}{y}\right)$，求 $\displaystyle x^{2}g_{xx}-y^{2}g_{yy}$。

<details>
<summary>查看答案与解析</summary>

<div className="exercise-label-row">
<strong>答案：</strong>
<div className="exercise-label-body">

$$
\boxed{
\displaystyle x^{2}g_{xx}-y^{2}g_{yy}
=2\dfrac{y}{x}f'\left(\dfrac{y}{x}\right)
}
$$

</div>
</div>

**解析：** 将

$$
\displaystyle g(x,y)=g_1(x,y)+g_2(x,y),
$$

其中

$$
\displaystyle g_1=f\left(\dfrac{y}{x}\right),
\qquad
g_2=yf\left(\dfrac{x}{y}\right).
$$

先计算 $g_1$。令 $\displaystyle r=\dfrac{y}{x}$，则

$$
\displaystyle r_x=-\dfrac{r}{x},
\qquad
r_{xx}=\dfrac{2r}{x^{2}},
\qquad
r_y=\dfrac{1}{x},
\qquad
r_{yy}=0.
$$

因此

$$
\displaystyle (g_1)_{xx}
=f''(r)\dfrac{r^{2}}{x^{2}}+f'(r)\dfrac{2r}{x^{2}},
$$

$$
\displaystyle (g_1)_{yy}=f''(r)\dfrac{1}{x^{2}}.
$$

注意 $\displaystyle y=rx$，所以

$$
\begin{aligned}
\displaystyle x^{2}(g_1)_{xx}-y^{2}(g_1)_{yy}
&=r^{2}f''(r)+2rf'(r)-r^{2}f''(r)\\
&=2rf'(r).
\end{aligned}
$$

再计算 $g_2$。令 $\displaystyle s=\dfrac{x}{y}$，则

$$
\displaystyle (g_2)_x=f'(s),
\qquad
(g_2)_{xx}=\dfrac{1}{y}f''(s).
$$

另一方面，

$$
\displaystyle (g_2)_y=f(s)-sf'(s),
$$

故

$$
\displaystyle (g_2)_{yy}=\dfrac{s^{2}}{y}f''(s).
$$

由 $\displaystyle x=sy$，得

$$
\begin{aligned}
\displaystyle x^{2}(g_2)_{xx}-y^{2}(g_2)_{yy}
&=\dfrac{x^{2}}{y}f''(s)-ys^{2}f''(s)\\
&=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

综上，

$$
\begin{aligned}
\displaystyle x^{2}g_{xx}-y^{2}g_{yy}
&=2rf'(r)\\
&=2\dfrac{y}{x}f'\left(\dfrac{y}{x}\right).
\end{aligned}
$$

</details>

9. **【2009-2-10 分】** 设 $\displaystyle z=f(x+y,x-y,xy)$，其中 $f$ 具有二阶连续偏导数，求 $\displaystyle dz$ 与 $\displaystyle \dfrac{\partial^{2}z}{\partial x\partial y}$。

<details>
<summary>查看答案与解析</summary>

**答案：** 令

$$
\displaystyle u=x+y,\qquad v=x-y,\qquad w=xy,
$$

则

$$
\boxed{
\displaystyle dz=\bigl(f_u+f_v+yf_w\bigr)\,dx+\bigl(f_u-f_v+xf_w\bigr)\,dy
}
$$

且

$$
\boxed{
\displaystyle z_{xy}=f_{uu}-f_{vv}+(x+y)f_{uw}+(x-y)f_{vw}+xyf_{ww}+f_w
}
$$

其中各偏导数均在点 $\displaystyle (u,v,w)=(x+y,x-y,xy)$ 处取值。

**解析：** 由复合函数全微分公式，

$$
\displaystyle du=dx+dy,\qquad dv=dx-dy,\qquad dw=y\,dx+x\,dy.
$$

因此

$$
\begin{aligned}
\displaystyle dz
&=f_u\,du+f_v\,dv+f_w\,dw\\
&=f_u(dx+dy)+f_v(dx-dy)+f_w(y\,dx+x\,dy)\\
&=\bigl(f_u+f_v+yf_w\bigr)dx
 +\bigl(f_u-f_v+xf_w\bigr)dy.
\end{aligned}
$$

先求

$$
\displaystyle z_x=f_u+f_v+yf_w.
$$

对 $y$ 再求偏导。由于

$$
\displaystyle u_y=1,\qquad v_y=-1,\qquad w_y=x,
$$

故

$$
\begin{aligned}
\displaystyle \frac{\partial f_u}{\partial y}
&=f_{uu}-f_{uv}+xf_{uw},\\
\displaystyle \frac{\partial f_v}{\partial y}
&=f_{vu}-f_{vv}+xf_{vw},\\
\displaystyle \frac{\partial (yf_w)}{\partial y}
&=f_w+y\bigl(f_{wu}-f_{wv}+xf_{ww}\bigr).
\end{aligned}
$$

因 $f$ 具有二阶连续偏导数，混合偏导可交换，即

$$
\displaystyle f_{uv}=f_{vu},\qquad f_{uw}=f_{wu},\qquad f_{vw}=f_{wv}.
$$

合并得

$$
\begin{aligned}
\displaystyle z_{xy}
&=f_{uu}-f_{vv}+xf_{uw}+xf_{vw}
+f_w+yf_{uw}-yf_{vw}+xyf_{ww}\\
&=f_{uu}-f_{vv}+(x+y)f_{uw}+(x-y)f_{vw}+xyf_{ww}+f_w.
\end{aligned}
$$

</details>

10. **【2010-2-11 分】** 设函数 $\displaystyle u=f(x,y)$ 具有二阶连续偏导数，且满足

$$
\displaystyle 4u_{xx}+12u_{xy}+5u_{yy}=0.
$$

确定 $a,b$ 的值，使该方程在变换 $\displaystyle \xi=x+ay$，$\displaystyle \eta=x+by$ 下化简为 $\displaystyle u_{\xi\eta}=0$。

<details>
<summary>查看答案与解析</summary>

<div className="exercise-label-row">
<strong>答案：</strong>
<div className="exercise-label-body">

$$
\boxed{
\displaystyle (a,b)=\left(-2,-\dfrac25\right)
\quad\text{或}\quad
(a,b)=\left(-\dfrac25,-2\right)
}
$$

</div>
</div>

**解析：** 将 $u$ 看成 $\displaystyle u=u(\xi,\eta)$。由

$$
\displaystyle \xi_x=1,\quad \eta_x=1,
\qquad
\xi_y=a,\quad \eta_y=b,
$$

可得微分算子

$$
\displaystyle \frac{\partial}{\partial x}
=\frac{\partial}{\partial \xi}+\frac{\partial}{\partial \eta},
\qquad
\frac{\partial}{\partial y}
=a\frac{\partial}{\partial \xi}+b\frac{\partial}{\partial \eta}.
$$

因此

$$
\displaystyle u_{xx}=u_{\xi\xi}+2u_{\xi\eta}+u_{\eta\eta},
$$

$$
\displaystyle u_{xy}=a u_{\xi\xi}+(a+b)u_{\xi\eta}+b u_{\eta\eta},
$$

$$
\displaystyle u_{yy}=a^{2}u_{\xi\xi}+2ab u_{\xi\eta}+b^{2}u_{\eta\eta}.
$$

代入原方程，$u_{\xi\xi}$ 与 $u_{\eta\eta}$ 的系数分别为

$$
\displaystyle 4+12a+5a^{2},
\qquad
4+12b+5b^{2}.
$$

要使方程只含混合偏导数，必须有

$$
\displaystyle 5a^{2}+12a+4=0,
\qquad
5b^{2}+12b+4=0.
$$

解二次方程

$$
\displaystyle 5t^{2}+12t+4=(5t+2)(t+2)=0,
$$

得

$$
\displaystyle t=-2\quad\text{或}\quad t=-\dfrac25.
$$

为保证变换可逆，应取 $a\ne b$，故 $a,b$ 分别取上述两个根。此时混合偏导项系数为

$$
\begin{aligned}
\displaystyle 8+12(a+b)+10ab
&=8+12\left(-\dfrac{12}{5}\right)+10\cdot\dfrac45\\
&=-\dfrac{64}{5}\ne0.
\end{aligned}
$$

所以原方程确实可化为

$$
\displaystyle u_{\xi\eta}=0.
$$

12. **【2011-12-10 分】** 设函数 $\displaystyle z=f\bigl(xy,yg(x)\bigr)$，其中 $f$ 具有二阶连续偏导数，函数 $g(x)$ 可导且在 $x=1$ 处取得极值，$\displaystyle g(1)=1$，求

$$
\displaystyle \left.\dfrac{\partial^{2}z}{\partial x\partial y}\right|_{x=1}.
$$

<details>
<summary>查看答案与解析</summary>

<div className="exercise-label-row">
<strong>答案：</strong>
<div className="exercise-label-body">

$$
\boxed{
\displaystyle \left.z_{xy}\right|_{x=1}
=f_u(y,y)+y f_{uu}(y,y)+y f_{uv}(y,y)
}
$$

</div>
</div>

**解析：** 令

$$
\displaystyle u=xy,
\qquad
v=yg(x),
\qquad
