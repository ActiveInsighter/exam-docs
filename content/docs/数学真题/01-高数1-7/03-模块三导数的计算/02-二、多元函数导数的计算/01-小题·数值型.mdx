# 小题·数值型

## 小题

### （一）数值型


<ExamQuestion>

1. **【1987-45-4 分】** 已知 $ z=\arctan \frac{x+y}{x-y}$，求 $ dz$。

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>
$$
 dz=\frac{-y\,dx+x\,dy}{x^{2}+y^{2}}.
$$
</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
令

$$
 u=\frac{x+y}{x-y},\qquad z=\arctan u.
$$

先求中间变量的偏导数：

$$
 \frac{\partial u}{\partial x}
=\frac{(x-y)-(x+y)}{(x-y)^{2}}
=-\frac{2y}{(x-y)^{2}},
$$

$$
 \frac{\partial u}{\partial y}
=\frac{(x-y)+(x+y)}{(x-y)^{2}}
=\frac{2x}{(x-y)^{2}}.
$$

又

$$
 1+u^{2}
=1+\left(\frac{x+y}{x-y}\right)^{2}
=\frac{2(x^{2}+y^{2})}{(x-y)^{2}}.
$$

因此

$$
 \frac{\partial z}{\partial x}
=\frac{1}{1+u^{2}}\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}
=-\frac{y}{x^{2}+y^{2}},
$$

$$
 \frac{\partial z}{\partial y}
=\frac{1}{1+u^{2}}\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}
=\frac{x}{x^{2}+y^{2}}.
$$

故全微分为

$$
\boxed{ dz
=\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\,dx
+\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}\,dy
=\frac{-y\,dx+x\,dy}{x^{2}+y^{2}}}.
$$

本题关键是使用复合函数求导法则，并先将 $ 1+u^{2}$ 通分化简。
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

2. **【1988-5-4 分】** 已知 $ u=e^{\frac{x}{y}}$，求 $ \frac{\partial^{2}u}{\partial x\partial y}$。

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>
$$
\boxed{
\frac{\partial^{2}u}{\partial x\partial y}
=-\frac{x+y}{y^{3}}e^{\frac{x}{y}}
}\qquad (y\ne0).
$$
</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
先对 $ x$ 求偏导：

$$

\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}
=\frac{1}{y}e^{\frac{x}{y}}.
$$

再对 $ y$ 求偏导。由乘积求导法则，

$$
\begin{aligned}

\frac{\partial^{2}u}{\partial x\partial y}
&=\frac{\partial}{\partial y}
\left(\frac{1}{y}e^{\frac{x}{y}}\right)\\
&=-\frac{1}{y^{2}}e^{\frac{x}{y}}
+\frac{1}{y}e^{\frac{x}{y}}
\left(-\frac{x}{y^{2}}\right)\\
&=-\left(\frac{1}{y^{2}}+\frac{x}{y^{3}}\right)e^{\frac{x}{y}}\\
&=-\frac{x+y}{y^{3}}e^{\frac{x}{y}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

函数及其偏导数要求 $ y\ne0$。
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

3. **【1989-5-5 分】** 已知 $ z=a^{\sqrt{x^{2}-y^{2}}}$，其中 $ a>0$，$ a\ne1$，求 $ dz$。

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>
$$
 dz
=\frac{a^{\sqrt{x^{2}-y^{2}}}\ln a}{\sqrt{x^{2}-y^{2}}}
(x\,dx-y\,dy).
$$
</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
令

$$
 u=\sqrt{x^{2}-y^{2}},
\qquad z=a^{u}.
$$

则

$$
 dz=a^{u}\ln a\,du.
$$

而

$$
\begin{aligned}
 du
&=\frac{1}{2\sqrt{x^{2}-y^{2}}}\,d(x^{2}-y^{2})\\
&=\frac{x\,dx-y\,dy}{\sqrt{x^{2}-y^{2}}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

故

$$
\boxed{ dz
=\frac{a^{\sqrt{x^{2}-y^{2}}}\ln a}{\sqrt{x^{2}-y^{2}}}
(x\,dx-y\,dy)}.
$$

在实数范围内使用上述微分公式时，应取 $ x^{2}-y^{2}>0$；边界处需另行讨论可微性。
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

4. **【1991-45-3 分】** 设 $ z=e^{\sin xy}$，则 $ dz=$

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>
$$
 dz=e^{\sin(xy)}\cos(xy)(y\,dx+x\,dy).
$$
</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
这是二重复合函数。令

$$
 u=xy,\qquad v=\sin u,\qquad z=e^{v}.
$$

则

$$
 dz=e^{v}\,dv,
\qquad
 dv=\cos u\,du,
$$

且

$$
 du=y\,dx+x\,dy.
$$

因此

$$
\boxed{ dz
=e^{\sin(xy)}\cos(xy)(y\,dx+x\,dy)}.
$$

等价地，

$$
 z_{x}=y e^{\sin(xy)}\cos(xy),
\qquad
z_{y}=x e^{\sin(xy)}\cos(xy).
$$
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

5. **【1994-45-5 分】** 已知

$$
 f(x,y)=x^{2}\arctan\frac{y}{x}-y^{2}\arctan\frac{x}{y},
$$

求 $ \frac{\partial^{2}f}{\partial x\partial y}$。

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>
$$
 \frac{\partial^{2}f}{\partial x\partial y}
=\frac{x^{2}-y^{2}}{x^{2}+y^{2}}.
$$
</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
先对 $ x$ 求偏导。注意

$$
 \frac{\partial}{\partial x}
\arctan\frac{y}{x}
=-\frac{y}{x^{2}+y^{2}},
$$

$$
 \frac{\partial}{\partial x}
\arctan\frac{x}{y}
=\frac{y}{x^{2}+y^{2}}.
$$

因此

$$
\begin{aligned}
 f_{x}
&=2x\arctan\frac{y}{x}
+x^{2}\left(-\frac{y}{x^{2}+y^{2}}\right)
-y^{2}\frac{y}{x^{2}+y^{2}}\\
&=2x\arctan\frac{y}{x}
-\frac{x^{2}y+y^{3}}{x^{2}+y^{2}}\\
&=2x\arctan\frac{y}{x}-y.
\end{aligned}
$$

再对 $ y$ 求偏导：

$$
\begin{aligned}
 f_{xy}
&=2x\cdot\frac{x}{x^{2}+y^{2}}-1\\
&=\frac{2x^{2}-(x^{2}+y^{2})}{x^{2}+y^{2}}\\
&=\frac{x^{2}-y^{2}}{x^{2}+y^{2}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

所以

$$
\boxed{ \frac{\partial^{2}f}{\partial x\partial y}
=\frac{x^{2}-y^{2}}{x^{2}+y^{2}}}.
$$

本题先求 $ f_{x}$ 后会出现显著约简，比分别对原式连续使用商法则更简洁。
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

6. **【1994-12-3 分】** 设

$$
 u=e^{-x}\sin\frac{x}{y},
$$

则 $ \frac{\partial^{2}u}{\partial x\partial y}$ 在 $ \left(2,\frac{1}{\pi}\right)$ 点处的值为 ______。

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>
$$
 \left.\frac{\partial^{2}u}{\partial x\partial y}
\right|_{\left(2,\frac{1}{\pi}\right)}
=\frac{\pi^{2}}{e^{2}}.
$$
</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
令

$$
 s=\frac{x}{y}.
$$

先对 $ x$ 求偏导：

$$
 u_{x}
=e^{-x}\left(-\sin s+\frac{1}{y}\cos s\right).
$$

再对 $ y$ 求偏导。由于

$$
 s_{y}=-\frac{x}{y^{2}},
$$

故

$$
\begin{aligned}
 u_{xy}
&=e^{-x}\left[
\frac{x}{y^{2}}\cos s
+\frac{x}{y^{3}}\sin s
-\frac{1}{y^{2}}\cos s
\right]\\
&=e^{-x}\left[
\frac{x-1}{y^{2}}\cos\frac{x}{y}
+\frac{x}{y^{3}}\sin\frac{x}{y}
\right].
\end{aligned}
$$

在点 $ \left(2,\frac{1}{\pi}\right)$，有

$$
 \frac{x}{y}=2\pi,
\qquad
\cos2\pi=1,
\qquad
\sin2\pi=0.
$$

所以

$$
\begin{aligned}
 u_{xy}
&=e^{-2}\cdot\frac{2-1}{(1/\pi)^{2}}\\
&=\frac{\pi^{2}}{e^{2}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

因此

$$
\boxed{ \left.u_{xy}\right|_{\left(2,\frac{1}{\pi}\right)}
=\frac{\pi^{2}}{e^{2}}}.
$$
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

$$
 z=(x^{2}+y^{2})e^{-\arctan\frac{y}{x}},
$$

求 $ dz$ 与 $ \frac{\partial^{2}z}{\partial x\partial y}$。

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>
$$
 dz=e^{-\arctan\frac{y}{x}}
\left[(2x+y)\,dx+(2y-x)\,dy\right],
$$

 \frac{\partial^{2}z}{\partial x\partial y}

\frac{y^{2}-x^{2}-xy}{x^{2}+y^{2}}.
$$
</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
记

$$
 \theta=\arctan\frac{y}{x}.

则

$$
 \theta_{x}=-\frac{y}{x^{2}+y^{2}},
\qquad
\theta_{y}=\frac{x}{x^{2}+y^{2}}.

对 $ z=(x^{2}+y^{2})e^{-\theta}$ 求偏导：

$$
\begin{aligned}
 z_{x}
&=2x e^{-\theta}-(x^{2}+y^{2})e^{-\theta}\theta_{x}\\
&=e^{-\theta}(2x+y),
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
 z_{y}
&=2y e^{-\theta}-(x^{2}+y^{2})e^{-\theta}\theta_{y}\\
&=e^{-\theta}(2y-x).
\end{aligned}
$$

因此

$$
\boxed{ dz=e^{-\arctan\frac{y}{x}}
\left[(2x+y)\,dx+(2y-x)\,dy\right]}.

再由 $ z_{x}=e^{-\theta}(2x+y)$ 对 $ y$ 求偏导：

$$
\begin{aligned}
 z_{xy}
&=e^{-\theta}
-e^{-\theta}\theta_{y}(2x+y)\\
&=e^{-\theta}
\left[1-\frac{x(2x+y)}{x^{2}+y^{2}}\right]\\
&=e^{-\theta}
\frac{y^{2}-x^{2}-xy}{x^{2}+y^{2}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

所以

$$
\boxed{ \frac{\partial^{2}z}{\partial x\partial y}
=e^{-\arctan\frac{y}{x}}
\frac{y^{2}-x^{2}-xy}{x^{2}+y^{2}}}.
$$
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

9. **【2008-2-4 分】** 设

$$
 z=\left(\frac{y}{x}\right)^{\frac{x}{y}},
$$

则 $ \left.\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\right|_{(1,2)}=$ ______。

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>
$$
 \left.\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\right|_{(1,2)}
=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}(\ln2-1).
$$
</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
对幂指函数采用对数求导。取对数得

$$
 \ln z=\frac{x}{y}\ln\frac{y}{x}.
$$

将 $ y$ 视为常数，对 $ x$ 求偏导：

$$
\begin{aligned}
 \frac{1}{z}z_{x}
&=\frac{1}{y}\ln\frac{y}{x}
+\frac{x}{y}\left(-\frac{1}{x}\right)\\
&=\frac{1}{y}\left(\ln\frac{y}{x}-1\right).
\end{aligned}
$$

因此

$$
 z_{x}
=\frac{z}{y}\left(\ln\frac{y}{x}-1\right).
$$

在点 $ (1,2)$ 处，

$$
 z(1,2)=2^{\frac{1}{2}}=\sqrt{2}.
$$

故

$$
\begin{aligned}
 z_{x}(1,2)
&=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}(\ln2-1).
\end{aligned}
$$

所以

$$
\boxed{ \left.\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\right|_{(1,2)}
=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}(\ln2-1)}.
$$

本题不能把 $ y/x$ 仅看作底数而忽略指数 $ x/y$ 对 $ x$ 的依赖；对数求导可同时处理两部分。
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

10. **【2008-23-4 分】** 设函数 $f$ 连续，若

$$
 F(u,v)=\iint_{D_{uv}}\frac{f(x^{2}+y^{2})}{\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}}}\,dx\,dy,
$$

其中区域 $D_{uv}$ 为图中阴影部分，则 $ \frac{\partial F}{\partial u}=$（　）

<ExamChoices>
<ExamOption>A. $ vf(u^{2})$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>B. $ \frac{v}{u}f(u^{2})$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>C. $ vf(u)$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>D. $ \frac{v}{u}f(u)$</ExamOption>
</ExamChoices>

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>A</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
由图可知，区域 $D_{uv}$ 在极坐标下可表示为

$$
 1\le r\le u,\qquad 0\le\theta\le v.
$$

令

$$
 x=r\cos\theta,\qquad y=r\sin\theta,
$$

则

$$
 x^{2}+y^{2}=r^{2},\qquad dx\,dy=r\,dr\,d\theta.
$$

因此

$$
\begin{aligned}
 F(u,v)
&=\int_{0}^{v}\int_{1}^{u}
\frac{f(r^{2})}{r}\cdot r\,dr\,d\theta\\
&=\int_{0}^{v}\int_{1}^{u}f(r^{2})\,dr\,d\theta\\
&=v\int_{1}^{u}f(r^{2})\,dr.
\end{aligned}
$$

由变上限积分求导公式，

$$
\boxed{ \frac{\partial F}{\partial u}=vf(u^{2})}.
$$

故选 A。选项 B 多出了因子 $ 1/u$；选项 C、D 则把 $ r=u$ 时的 $r^{2}$ 错写成了 $u$。
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

11. **【2009-3-4 分】** 设

$$
 z=(x+e^{y})^{x},
$$

则 $ \left.\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\right|_{(1,0)}=$ ______。

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>
$$
 1+2\ln2.
$$
</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>

$$
 \ln z=x\ln(x+e^{y}),
$$

将 $y$ 看作常数，对 $x$ 求偏导，得

$$
\begin{aligned}
 \frac{z_{x}}{z}
&=\ln(x+e^{y})+x\frac{1}{x+e^{y}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

故

$$
 z_{x}=(x+e^{y})^{x}
\left[\ln(x+e^{y})+\frac{x}{x+e^{y}}\right].
$$

在点 $ (1,0)$ 处，

$$
 z(1,0)=(1+1)^{1}=2,
$$

于是

$$
\begin{aligned}
 z_{x}(1,0)
&=2\left(\ln2+\frac12\right)\\
&=1+2\ln2.
\end{aligned}
$$

所以

$$
\boxed{ \left.\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\right|_{(1,0)}=1+2\ln2}.
$$
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

14. **【2016-23-4 分】** 已知函数

$$
 f(x,y)=\frac{e^{x}}{x-y},
$$

则（　）

<ExamChoices>
<ExamOption>A. $ f_{x}'-f_{y}'=0$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>B. $ f_{x}'+f_{y}'=0$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>C. $ f_{x}'-f_{y}'=f$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>D. $ f_{x}'+f_{y}'=f$</ExamOption>

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>D</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>

 f_{x}'
&=\frac{e^{x}}{x-y}-\frac{e^{x}}{(x-y)^{2}}.

 \frac{\partial}{\partial y}(x-y)^{-1}=(x-y)^{-2},

$$
\begin{aligned}
 f_{x}'+f_{y}'
&=\frac{e^{x}}{x-y}\\
&=f(x,y).
\end{aligned}
$$

所以

$$
\boxed{ f_{x}'+f_{y}'=f},
$$

故选 D。
</ExamExplanation>

 f_{xy}=f_{yx}.

$$
\boxed{ f_{xy}(1,1)=e(1+3)=4e}.

17. **【2020-23-4 分】** 设

$$
 z=\arctan\bigl[xy+\sin(x+y)\bigr],
$$

则 $ dz\big|_{(0,\pi)}=$ ______。

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>
$$
 dz\big|_{(0,\pi)}=(\pi-1)\,dx-dy.
$$
</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
令

$$
 g(x,y)=xy+\sin(x+y),
\qquad z=\arctan g.
$$

则

$$
 dz=\frac{1}{1+g^{2}}\,dg,
$$

其中

$$
 dg=\bigl[y+\cos(x+y)\bigr]dx
+\bigl[x+\cos(x+y)\bigr]dy.
$$

在点 $ (0,\pi)$ 处，

$$
 g(0,\pi)=\sin\pi=0,
$$

$$
 g_{x}(0,\pi)=\pi+\cos\pi=\pi-1,
$$

$$
 g_{y}(0,\pi)=0+\cos\pi=-1.
$$

所以

$$
\boxed{ dz\big|_{(0,\pi)}=(\pi-1)\,dx-dy}.
$$
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

18. **【2021-23-5 分】** 设函数 $f(x,y)$ 可微，且

$$
 f(x+1,e^{x})=x(x+1)^{2},
$$

$$
 f(x,x^{2})=2x^{2}\ln x,
$$

则 $ df(1,1)=$（　）

<ExamChoices>
<ExamOption>A. $ dx+dy$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>B. $ dx-dy$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>C. $ dy$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>D. $ -dy$</ExamOption>
</ExamChoices>

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>C</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
设

$$
 f_{x}(1,1)=A,
\qquad
f_{y}(1,1)=B.
$$

对第一个恒等式

$$
 f(x+1,e^{x})=x(x+1)^{2}
$$

两边对 $x$ 求导：

$$
 f_{x}(x+1,e^{x})
+e^{x}f_{y}(x+1,e^{x})
=(x+1)^{2}+2x(x+1).
$$

令 $ x=0$，对应点为 $ (1,1)$，得

$$
 A+B=1. \tag{1}
$$

对第二个恒等式

$$
 f(x,x^{2})=2x^{2}\ln x
$$

两边对 $x$ 求导：

$$
 f_{x}(x,x^{2})+2x f_{y}(x,x^{2})
=4x\ln x+2x.
$$

令 $ x=1$，得

$$
 A+2B=2. \tag{2}
$$

联立（1）（2），解得

$$
 A=0,
\qquad B=1.
$$

因此

$$
\begin{aligned}
 df(1,1)
&=f_{x}(1,1)\,dx+f_{y}(1,1)\,dy\\
&=dy.
\end{aligned}
$$

所以

$$
\boxed{ df(1,1)=dy},
$$

故选 C。
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

19. **【2022-23-5 分】** 已知 $f(x)$ 连续，令

$$
 F(x,y)=\int_{0}^{x-y}(x-y-t)f(t)\,dt,
$$

则（　）

<ExamChoices>
<ExamOption>
A.
$$
 \frac{\partial F}{\partial x}=\frac{\partial F}{\partial y},\quad
\frac{\partial^{2}F}{\partial x^{2}}=\frac{\partial^{2}F}{\partial y^{2}}
$$
</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>
B.
$$
 \frac{\partial F}{\partial x}=\frac{\partial F}{\partial y},\quad
\frac{\partial^{2}F}{\partial x^{2}}=-\frac{\partial^{2}F}{\partial y^{2}}
$$
</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>
C.
$$
 \frac{\partial F}{\partial x}=-\frac{\partial F}{\partial y},\quad
\frac{\partial^{2}F}{\partial x^{2}}=\frac{\partial^{2}F}{\partial y^{2}}
$$
</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>
D.

$$
 \frac{\partial F}{\partial x}=-\frac{\partial F}{\partial y},\quad
\frac{\partial^{2}F}{\partial x^{2}}=-\frac{\partial^{2}F}{\partial y^{2}}
$$
</ExamOption>
</ExamChoices>

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>C</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
令

$$
 s=x-y,
$$

并记

$$
 H(s)=\int_{0}^{s}(s-t)f(t)\,dt.
$$

则

$$
 F(x,y)=H(x-y).
$$

由莱布尼茨公式，

$$
\begin{aligned}
 H'(s)
&=(s-s)f(s)+\int_{0}^{s}f(t)\,dt\\
&=\int_{0}^{s}f(t)\,dt,
\end{aligned}
$$

从而

$$
 H''(s)=f(s).
$$

利用复合函数求导，

$$
 F_{x}=H'(x-y),
\qquad
F_{y}=-H'(x-y),
$$

故

$$
 F_{x}=-F_{y}.
$$

再求二阶偏导：

$$
 F_{xx}=H''(x-y)=f(x-y),
$$

$$
 F_{yy}=H''(x-y)=f(x-y).
$$

所以

$$
\boxed{ F_{x}=-F_{y},\qquad F_{xx}=F_{yy}},
$$

故选 C。
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

20. **【2023-3-5 分】** 已知函数 $f(x,y)$ 满足

$$
 df(x,y)=\frac{x\,dy-y\,dx}{x^{2}+y^{2}},
$$

且 $ f(1,1)=\frac{\pi}{4}$，则 $ f(\sqrt{3},3)=$

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>
$$
 \frac{\pi}{3}.
$$
</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
注意到

$$
\begin{aligned}
 d\!\left(\arctan\frac{y}{x}\right)
&=\frac{1}{1+(y/x)^{2}}\,d\!\left(\frac{y}{x}\right)\\
&=\frac{x\,dy-y\,dx}{x^{2}+y^{2}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

因此在点 $ (1,1)$ 所在的第一象限内，

$$
 f(x,y)=\arctan\frac{y}{x}+C.
$$

由 $ f(1,1)=\frac{\pi}{4}$ 得

$$
 \frac{\pi}{4}+C=\frac{\pi}{4},
$$

故 $ C=0$。于是

$$
\begin{aligned}
 f(\sqrt{3},3)
&=\arctan\frac{3}{\sqrt{3}}\\
&=\arctan\sqrt{3}\\
&=\frac{\pi}{3}.
\end{aligned}
$$
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

21. **【2024-1-5 分】** 已知 $f(u,v)$ 存在二阶连续的偏导数，且

$$
 df(1,1)=3\,du+4\,dv.
$$

若 $ y=f(\cos x,1+x^{2})$，则

$$
 \left.\frac{dy}{dx}\right|_{x=0}=\underline{\qquad}.
$$

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>
$$
 0.
$$
</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
由全微分的定义可知

$$
 f_{u}(1,1)=3,
\qquad
f_{v}(1,1)=4.
$$

令

$$
 u=\cos x,
\qquad
v=1+x^{2},
$$

则由复合函数求导法则，

$$
 \frac{dy}{dx}
=f_{u}(u,v)(-\sin x)+f_{v}(u,v)\cdot2x.
$$

当 $ x=0$ 时，$ (u,v)=(1,1)$，且

$$
 -\sin0=0,
\qquad
2\cdot0=0.
$$

因此

$$
 \left.\frac{dy}{dx}\right|_{x=0}
=3\cdot0+4\cdot0=0.
$$
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>
