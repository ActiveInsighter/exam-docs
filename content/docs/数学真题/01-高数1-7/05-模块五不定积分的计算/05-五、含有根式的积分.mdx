# 五、含有根式的积分

## 小题

1. **【1987-4-4 分】** 求不定积分

$$
\displaystyle \int e^{\sqrt{2x-1}}\,dx.
$$

<details>
<summary>查看答案与解析</summary>

<div className="exercise-label-row">
<strong>答案：</strong>
<div className="exercise-label-body">

$$
\displaystyle \int e^{\sqrt{2x-1}}\,dx
=\left(\sqrt{2x-1}-1\right)e^{\sqrt{2x-1}}+C.
$$

</div>
</div>

**解析：** 被积函数的指数中含有根式，令

$$
  \displaystyle t=\sqrt{2x-1}.
$$

则

$$
  \displaystyle t^2=2x-1,
  \qquad x=\dfrac{t^2+1}{2},
  \qquad dx=t\,dt.
$$

原积分化为

$$
  \begin{aligned}
  \displaystyle \int e^{\sqrt{2x-1}}\,dx
  &=\int te^t\,dt\\
  &=te^t-\int e^t\,dt\\
  &=(t-1)e^t+C.
  \end{aligned}
$$

代回 $\displaystyle t=\sqrt{2x-1}$，得

$$
  \displaystyle \int e^{\sqrt{2x-1}}\,dx
=\left(\sqrt{2x-1}-1\right)e^{\sqrt{2x-1}}+C.
$$

本题的关键是用根式整体作换元，随后对 $\displaystyle \int te^t\,dt$ 使用分部积分。

</details>

2. **【1992-3-5 分】** 求

$$
\displaystyle \int \dfrac{x^3}{\sqrt{1+x^2}}\,dx.
$$

<details>
<summary>查看答案与解析</summary>

<div className="exercise-label-row">
<strong>答案：</strong>
<div className="exercise-label-body">

$$
\displaystyle \int \dfrac{x^3}{\sqrt{1+x^2}}\,dx
=\dfrac13\left(1+x^2\right)^{\frac32}-\sqrt{1+x^2}+C.
$$

</div>
</div>

也可写为

$$
\displaystyle \dfrac{x^2-2}{3}\sqrt{1+x^2}+C.
$$

**解析：** 注意到分母含有 $\displaystyle \sqrt{1+x^2}$，且分子可以拆成

$$
  \displaystyle x^3\,dx=x^2\cdot x\,dx.
$$

令

$$
  \displaystyle u=1+x^2,
  \qquad du=2x\,dx,
  \qquad x^2=u-1.
$$

则

$$
  \begin{aligned}
  \displaystyle \int \dfrac{x^3}{\sqrt{1+x^2}}\,dx
  &=\dfrac12\int \dfrac{u-1}{\sqrt u}\,du\\
  &=\dfrac12\int\left(u^{\frac12}-u^{-\frac12}\right)du\\
  &=\dfrac13u^{\frac32}-u^{\frac12}+C.
  \end{aligned}
$$

代回 $\displaystyle u=1+x^2$，得到

$$
  \displaystyle \int \dfrac{x^3}{\sqrt{1+x^2}}\,dx
=\dfrac13\left(1+x^2\right)^{\frac32}-\sqrt{1+x^2}+C.
$$

提取 $\displaystyle \sqrt{1+x^2}$ 后，还可化为

$$
  \displaystyle \dfrac{x^2-2}{3}\sqrt{1+x^2}+C.
$$

</details>

3. **【1993-5-3 分】**

$$
\displaystyle \int \dfrac{dx}{(2-x)\sqrt{1-x}}=\underline{\qquad}.
$$

<details>
<summary>查看答案与解析</summary>

<div className="exercise-label-row">
<strong>答案：</strong>
<div className="exercise-label-body">

$$
\displaystyle \int \dfrac{dx}{(2-x)\sqrt{1-x}}
=-2\arctan\sqrt{1-x}+C.
$$

</div>
</div>

**解析：** 令

$$
  \displaystyle t=\sqrt{1-x},
$$

则

$$
  \displaystyle x=1-t^2,
  \qquad dx=-2t\,dt,
  \qquad 2-x=1+t^2.
$$

因而

$$
  \begin{aligned}
  \displaystyle \int \dfrac{dx}{(2-x)\sqrt{1-x}}
  &=\int \dfrac{-2t\,dt}{(1+t^2)t}\\
  &=-2\int\dfrac{dt}{1+t^2}\\
  &=-2\arctan t+C.
  \end{aligned}
$$

代回 $\displaystyle t=\sqrt{1-x}$，即得

$$
  \displaystyle -2\arctan\sqrt{1-x}+C.
$$

由于原被积函数含有 $\displaystyle \sqrt{1-x}$ 且该根式位于分母，因此实数范围内原函数应在区间 $\displaystyle (-\infty,1)$ 上理解。

</details>

4. **【1997-2-3 分】**

$$
\displaystyle \int \dfrac{dx}{\sqrt{x(4-x)}}=\underline{\qquad}.
$$

<details>
<summary>查看答案与解析</summary>

<div className="exercise-label-row">
<strong>答案：</strong>
<div className="exercise-label-body">

$$
\displaystyle \int \dfrac{dx}{\sqrt{x(4-x)}}
=\arcsin\left(\dfrac{x-2}{2}\right)+C.
$$

</div>
</div>

**解析：** 先将根式中的二次式配方：

$$
  \begin{aligned}
  \displaystyle x(4-x)
  &=4x-x^2\\
  &=4-(x-2)^2.
  \end{aligned}
$$

因此原积分为

$$
  \displaystyle \int\dfrac{dx}{\sqrt{4-(x-2)^2}}.
$$

令

$$
  \displaystyle u=\dfrac{x-2}{2},
  \qquad dx=2\,du.
$$

则

$$
  \begin{aligned}
  \displaystyle \int\dfrac{dx}{\sqrt{4-(x-2)^2}}
  &=\int\dfrac{2\,du}{2\sqrt{1-u^2}}\\
  &=\int\dfrac{du}{\sqrt{1-u^2}}\\
  &=\arcsin u+C.
  \end{aligned}
$$

代回后得到

$$
  \displaystyle \arcsin\left(\dfrac{x-2}{2}\right)+C.
$$

原被积函数在实数范围内的连续区间为 $\displaystyle (0,4)$。答案也可写成 $\displaystyle 2\arcsin\dfrac{\sqrt{x}}{2}+C$，两种形式在该区间内只相差常数。

</details>

## 大题

1. **【2001-2-6 分】** 求

$$
\displaystyle \int \dfrac{dx}{(2x^2+1)\sqrt{x^2+1}}.
$$

<details>
<summary>查看答案与解析</summary>

<div className="exercise-label-row">
<strong>答案：</strong>
<div className="exercise-label-body">

$$
\displaystyle \int \dfrac{dx}{(2x^2+1)\sqrt{x^2+1}}
=\arctan\left(\dfrac{x}{\sqrt{x^2+1}}\right)+C.
$$

</div>
</div>

**解析：** 观察到 $\displaystyle \dfrac{x}{\sqrt{x^2+1}}$ 的导数能够产生 $\displaystyle (x^2+1)^{-\frac32}$，令

$$
  \displaystyle t=\dfrac{x}{\sqrt{x^2+1}}.
$$

对其求导，得

$$
  \begin{aligned}
  \displaystyle dt
  &=\left[x(x^2+1)^{-\frac12}\right]'dx\\
  &=\left[(x^2+1)^{-\frac12}-x^2(x^2+1)^{-\frac32}\right]dx\\
  &=\dfrac{dx}{(x^2+1)^{\frac32}}.
  \end{aligned}
$$

又由

$$
  \displaystyle t^2=\dfrac{x^2}{x^2+1}
$$

可得

$$
  \begin{aligned}
  \displaystyle 1+t^2
  &=1+\dfrac{x^2}{x^2+1}\\
  &=\dfrac{2x^2+1}{x^2+1}.
  \end{aligned}
$$

将原被积式改写为

$$
  \begin{aligned}
  \displaystyle \dfrac{dx}{(2x^2+1)\sqrt{x^2+1}}
  &=\dfrac{x^2+1}{2x^2+1}\cdot
  \dfrac{dx}{(x^2+1)^{\frac32}}\\
  &=\dfrac{dt}{1+t^2}.
  \end{aligned}
$$

因此

$$
  \begin{aligned}
  \displaystyle \int \dfrac{dx}{(2x^2+1)\sqrt{x^2+1}}
  &=\int\dfrac{dt}{1+t^2}\\
  &=\arctan t+C\\
  &=\arctan\left(\dfrac{x}{\sqrt{x^2+1}}\right)+C.
  \end{aligned}
$$

验算时，利用

$$
  \displaystyle \left(\arctan t\right)'=\dfrac{t'}{1+t^2}
$$

即可直接还原原被积函数。本题的关键是选择能够同时化简根式 $\displaystyle \sqrt{x^2+1}$ 与二次因子 $\displaystyle 2x^2+1$ 的换元。

</details>
