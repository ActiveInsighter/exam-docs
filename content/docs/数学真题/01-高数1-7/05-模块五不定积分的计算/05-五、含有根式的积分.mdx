# 五、含有根式的积分

## 小题

<ExamQuestion>

1. **【1987-4-4 分】** 求不定积分

$$
 \int e^{\sqrt{2x-1}}\,dx.
$$

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>
$$
 \int e^{\sqrt{2x-1}}\,dx
=\left(\sqrt{2x-1}-1\right)e^{\sqrt{2x-1}}+C.
$$
</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
被积函数的指数中含有根式，令

$$
   t=\sqrt{2x-1}.
$$

则

$$
   t^2=2x-1,
  \qquad x=\frac{t^2+1}{2},
  \qquad dx=t\,dt.
$$

原积分化为

$$
  \begin{aligned}
   \int e^{\sqrt{2x-1}}\,dx
  &=\int te^t\,dt\\
  &=te^t-\int e^t\,dt\\
  &=(t-1)e^t+C.
  \end{aligned}
$$

代回 $ t=\sqrt{2x-1}$，得

$$
   \int e^{\sqrt{2x-1}}\,dx
=\left(\sqrt{2x-1}-1\right)e^{\sqrt{2x-1}}+C.
$$

本题的关键是用根式整体作换元，随后对 $ \int te^t\,dt$ 使用分部积分。
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

<ExamQuestion>

2. **【1992-3-5 分】** 求

$$
 \int \frac{x^3}{\sqrt{1+x^2}}\,dx.
$$

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>
$$
 \int \frac{x^3}{\sqrt{1+x^2}}\,dx
=\frac13\left(1+x^2\right)^{\frac32}-\sqrt{1+x^2}+C.
$$




也可写为

$$
 \frac{x^2-2}{3}\sqrt{1+x^2}+C.
$$
</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
注意到分母含有 $ \sqrt{1+x^2}$，且分子可以拆成

$$
   x^3\,dx=x^2\cdot x\,dx.
$$

令

$$
   u=1+x^2,
  \qquad du=2x\,dx,
  \qquad x^2=u-1.
$$

则

$$
  \begin{aligned}
   \int \frac{x^3}{\sqrt{1+x^2}}\,dx
  &=\frac12\int \frac{u-1}{\sqrt u}\,du\\
  &=\frac12\int\left(u^{\frac12}-u^{-\frac12}\right)du\\
  &=\frac13u^{\frac32}-u^{\frac12}+C.
  \end{aligned}
$$

代回 $ u=1+x^2$，得到

$$
   \int \frac{x^3}{\sqrt{1+x^2}}\,dx
=\frac13\left(1+x^2\right)^{\frac32}-\sqrt{1+x^2}+C.
$$

提取 $ \sqrt{1+x^2}$ 后，还可化为

$$
   \frac{x^2-2}{3}\sqrt{1+x^2}+C.
$$
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

<ExamQuestion>

3. **【1993-5-3 分】**

$$
 \int \frac{dx}{(2-x)\sqrt{1-x}}=\underline{\qquad}.
$$

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>
$$
 \int \frac{dx}{(2-x)\sqrt{1-x}}
=-2\arctan\sqrt{1-x}+C.
$$
</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
令

$$
   t=\sqrt{1-x},
$$

则

$$
   x=1-t^2,
  \qquad dx=-2t\,dt,
  \qquad 2-x=1+t^2.
$$

因而

$$
  \begin{aligned}
   \int \frac{dx}{(2-x)\sqrt{1-x}}
  &=\int \frac{-2t\,dt}{(1+t^2)t}\\
  &=-2\int\frac{dt}{1+t^2}\\
  &=-2\arctan t+C.
  \end{aligned}
$$

代回 $ t=\sqrt{1-x}$，即得

$$
   -2\arctan\sqrt{1-x}+C.
$$

由于原被积函数含有 $ \sqrt{1-x}$ 且该根式位于分母，因此实数范围内原函数应在区间 $ (-\infty,1)$ 上理解。
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

<ExamQuestion>

4. **【1997-2-3 分】**

$$
 \int \frac{dx}{\sqrt{x(4-x)}}=\underline{\qquad}.
$$

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>
$$
 \int \frac{dx}{\sqrt{x(4-x)}}
=\arcsin\left(\frac{x-2}{2}\right)+C.
$$
</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
先将根式中的二次式配方：

$$
  \begin{aligned}
   x(4-x)
  &=4x-x^2\\
  &=4-(x-2)^2.
  \end{aligned}
$$

因此原积分为

$$
   \int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{4-(x-2)^2}}.
$$

令

$$
   u=\frac{x-2}{2},
  \qquad dx=2\,du.
$$

则

$$
  \begin{aligned}
   \int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{4-(x-2)^2}}
  &=\int\frac{2\,du}{2\sqrt{1-u^2}}\\
  &=\int\frac{du}{\sqrt{1-u^2}}\\
  &=\arcsin u+C.
  \end{aligned}
$$

代回后得到

$$
   \arcsin\left(\frac{x-2}{2}\right)+C.
$$

原被积函数在实数范围内的连续区间为 $ (0,4)$。答案也可写成 $ 2\arcsin\frac{\sqrt{x}}{2}+C$，两种形式在该区间内只相差常数。
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

## 大题

<ExamQuestion>

1. **【2001-2-6 分】** 求

$$
 \int \frac{dx}{(2x^2+1)\sqrt{x^2+1}}.
$$

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>
$$
 \int \frac{dx}{(2x^2+1)\sqrt{x^2+1}}
=\arctan\left(\frac{x}{\sqrt{x^2+1}}\right)+C.
$$
</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
观察到 $ \frac{x}{\sqrt{x^2+1}}$ 的导数能够产生 $ (x^2+1)^{-\frac32}$，令

$$
   t=\frac{x}{\sqrt{x^2+1}}.
$$

对其求导，得

$$
  \begin{aligned}
   dt
  &=\left[x(x^2+1)^{-\frac12}\right]'dx\\
  &=\left[(x^2+1)^{-\frac12}-x^2(x^2+1)^{-\frac32}\right]dx\\
  &=\frac{dx}{(x^2+1)^{\frac32}}.
  \end{aligned}
$$

又由

$$
   t^2=\frac{x^2}{x^2+1}
$$

可得

$$
  \begin{aligned}
   1+t^2
  &=1+\frac{x^2}{x^2+1}\\
  &=\frac{2x^2+1}{x^2+1}.
  \end{aligned}
$$

将原被积式改写为

$$
  \begin{aligned}
   \frac{dx}{(2x^2+1)\sqrt{x^2+1}}
  &=\frac{x^2+1}{2x^2+1}\cdot
  \frac{dx}{(x^2+1)^{\frac32}}\\
  &=\frac{dt}{1+t^2}.
  \end{aligned}
$$

因此

$$
  \begin{aligned}
   \int \frac{dx}{(2x^2+1)\sqrt{x^2+1}}
  &=\int\frac{dt}{1+t^2}\\
  &=\arctan t+C\\
  &=\arctan\left(\frac{x}{\sqrt{x^2+1}}\right)+C.
  \end{aligned}
$$

验算时，利用

$$
   \left(\arctan t\right)'=\frac{t'}{1+t^2}
$$

即可直接还原原被积函数。本题的关键是选择能够同时化简根式 $ \sqrt{x^2+1}$ 与二次因子 $ 2x^2+1$ 的换元。
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>
