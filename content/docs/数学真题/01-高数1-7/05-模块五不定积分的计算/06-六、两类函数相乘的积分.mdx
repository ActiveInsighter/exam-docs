# 六、两类函数相乘的积分

## 小题

1. **【1989-5-5 分】** 求不定积分

$$
\displaystyle \int \dfrac{x+\ln(1-x)}{x^2}\,dx.
$$

<details>
<summary>查看答案与解析</summary>

<div className="exercise-label-row">
<strong>答案：</strong>
<div className="exercise-label-body">

$$
\displaystyle \int \dfrac{x+\ln(1-x)}{x^2}\,dx
=\ln(1-x)-\dfrac{\ln(1-x)}{x}+C.
$$

</div>
</div>

**解析：** 先将积分拆分为

$$
  \displaystyle I=\int\dfrac{dx}{x}+\int\dfrac{\ln(1-x)}{x^2}\,dx.
$$

对第二项使用分部积分，取

$$
  \displaystyle u=\ln(1-x),\qquad dv=\dfrac{dx}{x^2},
$$

则

$$
  \displaystyle du=-\dfrac{dx}{1-x},\qquad v=-\dfrac1x.
$$

因而

$$
  \begin{aligned}
  \displaystyle \int\dfrac{\ln(1-x)}{x^2}\,dx
  &=-\dfrac{\ln(1-x)}x-\int\dfrac{dx}{x(1-x)}\\
  &=-\dfrac{\ln(1-x)}x-\int\left(\dfrac1x+\dfrac1{1-x}\right)dx\\
  &=-\dfrac{\ln(1-x)}x-\ln|x|+\ln(1-x)+C.
  \end{aligned}
$$

再与第一项 $\displaystyle \int\dfrac{dx}{x}=\ln|x|$ 相加，$\ln|x|$ 正好抵消，得到

$$
  \displaystyle I=\ln(1-x)-\dfrac{\ln(1-x)}x+C.
$$

由于原式含有 $\ln(1-x)$，其实数定义域要求 $x<1$，同时还需 $x\ne0$。

</details>

2. **【1989-3-4 分】** 求

$$
\displaystyle \int \dfrac{dx}{x\ln^2x}.
$$

<details>
<summary>查看答案与解析</summary>

<div className="exercise-label-row">
<strong>答案：</strong>
<div className="exercise-label-body">

$$
\displaystyle \int \dfrac{dx}{x\ln^2x}=-\dfrac1{\ln x}+C.
$$

</div>
</div>

**解析：** 令

$$
  \displaystyle u=\ln x,
$$

则

$$
  \displaystyle du=\dfrac{dx}{x}.
$$

因此

$$
  \begin{aligned}
  \displaystyle \int\dfrac{dx}{x\ln^2x}
  &=\int u^{-2}\,du\\
  &=-u^{-1}+C\\
  &=-\dfrac1{\ln x}+C.
  \end{aligned}
$$

本题使用的是复合函数积分公式，原被积函数的定义要求 $x>0$ 且 $x\ne1$。

</details>

3. **【1990-3-5 分】** 计算

$$
\displaystyle \int \dfrac{\ln x}{(1-x)^2}\,dx.
$$

<details>
<summary>查看答案与解析</summary>

<div className="exercise-label-row">
<strong>答案：</strong>
<div className="exercise-label-body">

$$
\displaystyle \int \dfrac{\ln x}{(1-x)^2}\,dx
=\dfrac{x\ln x}{1-x}+\ln|1-x|+C.
$$

</div>
</div>

**解析：** 取

$$
  \displaystyle u=\ln x,\qquad dv=\dfrac{dx}{(1-x)^2},
$$

则

$$
  \displaystyle du=\dfrac{dx}{x},\qquad v=\dfrac1{1-x}.
$$

由分部积分公式，

$$
  \begin{aligned}
  \displaystyle I
  &=\dfrac{\ln x}{1-x}-\int\dfrac{dx}{x(1-x)}\\
  &=\dfrac{\ln x}{1-x}-\int\left(\dfrac1x+\dfrac1{1-x}\right)dx\\
  &=\dfrac{\ln x}{1-x}-\ln x+\ln|1-x|+C.
  \end{aligned}
$$

合并前两项：

$$
  \displaystyle \dfrac{\ln x}{1-x}-\ln x
=\dfrac{x\ln x}{1-x},
$$

故

$$
  \displaystyle I=\dfrac{x\ln x}{1-x}+\ln|1-x|+C.
$$

</details>

4. **【1990-5-5 分】** 求不定积分

$$
\displaystyle \int \dfrac{x\cos^4\dfrac{x}{2}}{\sin^3x}\,dx.
$$

<details>
<summary>查看答案与解析</summary>

<div className="exercise-label-row">
<strong>答案：</strong>
<div className="exercise-label-body">

$$
\displaystyle \int \dfrac{x\cos^4\dfrac{x}{2}}{\sin^3x}\,dx
=-\dfrac{x}{8}\cot^2\dfrac{x}{2}-\dfrac14\cot\dfrac{x}{2}-\dfrac{x}{8}+C.
$$

</div>
</div>

**解析：** 利用

$$
  \displaystyle \sin x=2\sin\dfrac{x}{2}\cos\dfrac{x}{2},
$$

有

$$
  \begin{aligned}
  \displaystyle \dfrac{\cos^4\dfrac{x}{2}}{\sin^3x}
  &=\dfrac{\cos^4\dfrac{x}{2}}{8\sin^3\dfrac{x}{2}\cos^3\dfrac{x}{2}}\\
  &=\dfrac18\cot\dfrac{x}{2}\csc^2\dfrac{x}{2}.
  \end{aligned}
$$

又因为

$$
  \displaystyle \dfrac{d}{dx}\left(\cot^2\dfrac{x}{2}\right)
=-\cot\dfrac{x}{2}\csc^2\dfrac{x}{2},
$$

所以

$$
  \displaystyle I=-\dfrac18\int x\,d\left(\cot^2\dfrac{x}{2}\right).
$$

分部积分得

$$
  \displaystyle I=-\dfrac{x}{8}\cot^2\dfrac{x}{2}
+\dfrac18\int\cot^2\dfrac{x}{2}\,dx.
$$

利用 $\displaystyle \cot^2u=\csc^2u-1$，可得

$$
  \begin{aligned}
  \displaystyle \int\cot^2\dfrac{x}{2}\,dx
  &=\int\left(\csc^2\dfrac{x}{2}-1\right)dx\\
  &=-2\cot\dfrac{x}{2}-x+C.
  \end{aligned}
$$

代回即得

$$
  \displaystyle I=-\dfrac{x}{8}\cot^2\dfrac{x}{2}
-\dfrac14\cot\dfrac{x}{2}-\dfrac{x}{8}+C.
$$

</details>

5. **【1991-5-5 分】** 求不定积分

$$
\displaystyle I=\int \dfrac{x^2}{1+x^2}\arctan x\,dx.
$$

<details>
<summary>查看答案与解析</summary>

<div className="exercise-label-row">
<strong>答案：</strong>
<div className="exercise-label-body">

$$
\displaystyle I=x\arctan x-\dfrac12\ln(1+x^2)
-\dfrac12(\arctan x)^2+C.
$$

</div>
</div>

**解析：** 先作恒等变形

$$
  \displaystyle \dfrac{x^2}{1+x^2}=1-\dfrac1{1+x^2}.
$$

因此

$$
  \displaystyle I=\int\arctan x\,dx
-\int\dfrac{\arctan x}{1+x^2}\,dx.
$$

第一项分部积分：

$$
  \begin{aligned}
  \displaystyle \int\arctan x\,dx
  &=x\arctan x-\int\dfrac{x}{1+x^2}\,dx\\
  &=x\arctan x-\dfrac12\ln(1+x^2).
  \end{aligned}
$$

第二项令 $\displaystyle u=\arctan x$，则 $\displaystyle du=\dfrac{dx}{1+x^2}$，故

$$
  \displaystyle \int\dfrac{\arctan x}{1+x^2}\,dx
=\dfrac12(\arctan x)^2.
$$

合并得到

$$
  \displaystyle I=x\arctan x-\dfrac12\ln(1+x^2)
-\dfrac12(\arctan x)^2+C.
$$

</details>

<summary>查看答案与解析</summary>

+\dfrac12\ln(1+e^{2x})+C.
$$

</div>
</div>

**解析：** 将原式写成

$$
  \displaystyle I=\int e^{-x}\operatorname{arccot}e^x\,dx.
$$

取

$$
  \displaystyle u=\operatorname{arccot}e^x,
  \qquad dv=e^{-x}dx,
$$

则

$$
  \displaystyle du=-\dfrac{e^x}{1+e^{2x}}dx,
  \qquad v=-e^{-x}.

$$
  \begin{aligned}
  \displaystyle I
  &=-e^{-x}\operatorname{arccot}e^x

$$
  \begin{aligned}

  &=x-\dfrac12\ln(1+e^{2x})+C.
  \end{aligned}
$$

因此

$$
  \displaystyle I=-e^{-x}\operatorname{arccot}e^x-x
+\dfrac12\ln(1+e^{2x})+C.
