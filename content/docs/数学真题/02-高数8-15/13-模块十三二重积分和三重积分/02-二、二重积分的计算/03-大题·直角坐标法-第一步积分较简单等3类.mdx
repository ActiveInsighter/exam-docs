### （二）直角坐标法：第一步积分较简单


<ExamQuestion>

1. **【2002-1-7 分】** $D=\{(x,y)\mid0\le x\le1,\ 0\le y\le1\}$，计算

$$
 \iint_D e^{\max(x^2,y^2)}\,dx\,dy.
$$

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>$ e-1$。</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
正方形 $D$ 关于直线 $y=x$ 对称。在三角形

$$
 D_1=\{(x,y)\mid0\le y\le x\le1\}
$$

内，有 $\max(x^2,y^2)=x^2$。由对称性，

$$
\begin{aligned}
 I
&=2\iint_{D_1}e^{x^2}\,dx\,dy\\
&=2\int_0^1dx\int_0^x e^{x^2}\,dy\\
&=2\int_0^1xe^{x^2}\,dx\\
&=\left.e^{x^2}\right|_0^1\\
&=e-1.
\end{aligned}
$$
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

2. **【2006-3-7 分】** $D$ 由 $y=x$，$y=1$，$x=0$ 围成，计算

$$
 \iint_D\sqrt{y^2-xy}\,dx\,dy.
$$

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>$ \frac29$。</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
区域可表示为

$$
 0\le y\le1,
\qquad 0\le x\le y.
$$

因为固定 $y$ 后对 $x$ 积分较简单，所以

$$
\begin{aligned}
 I
&=\int_0^1dy\int_0^y\sqrt{y(y-x)}\,dx.
\end{aligned}
$$

令 $u=y-x$，则 $dx=-du$，从而

$$
\begin{aligned}
 \int_0^y\sqrt{y(y-x)}\,dx
&=\sqrt y\int_0^y\sqrt u\,du\\
&=\sqrt y\cdot\frac23y^{3/2}\\
&=\frac23y^2.
\end{aligned}
$$

故

$$
 I
=\frac23\int_0^1y^2\,dy
=\frac23\cdot\frac13
=\frac29.
$$
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

3. **【2013-23-10 分】** $D$ 由 $x=3y$，$y=3x$，$x+y=8$ 围成，计算

$$
 \iint_Dx^2\,dx\,dy.
$$

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>$ \frac{416}{3}$。</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
三条直线的交点分别为

$$
 (0,0),\qquad(6,2),\qquad(2,6).
$$

按 $x$ 的范围分割区域：

$$
\begin{cases}
 0\le x\le2,
& \frac{x}{3}\le y\le3x,\\[2mm]
 2\le x\le6,
& \frac{x}{3}\le y\le8-x.
\end{cases}
$$

于是

$$
\begin{aligned}
 I
&=\int_0^2dx\int_{x/3}^{3x}x^2\,dy
+\int_2^6dx\int_{x/3}^{8-x}x^2\,dy\\
&=\int_0^2x^2\left(3x-\frac{x}{3}\right)dx
+\int_2^6x^2\left(8-x-\frac{x}{3}\right)dx\\
&=\frac83\int_0^2x^3\,dx
+\int_2^6\left(8x^2-\frac43x^3\right)dx\\
&=\frac{32}{3}+128\\
&=\frac{416}{3}.
\end{aligned}
$$
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

4. **【2017-3-10 分】** $D$ 位于第一象限，边界为 $y=\sqrt{x}$ 与 $y$ 轴，计算

$$
 \iint_D \frac{y^3}{(1+x^2+y^4)^2}\,dx\,dy.
$$

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>$ \frac{\pi}{8\sqrt2}$。</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
在第一象限内，曲线 $y=\sqrt{x}$ 等价于 $x=y^2$。由该曲线与 $y$ 轴所确定的无界区域为

$$
 D=\{(x,y)\mid x\ge0,\ y\ge\sqrt{x}\}.
$$

若先对 $y$ 积分，则可直接令 $u=1+x^2+y^4$，因此写成

$$
\begin{aligned}
 I
&=\int_0^{+\infty}dx\int_{\sqrt{x}}^{+\infty}
\frac{y^3}{(1+x^2+y^4)^2}\,dy.
\end{aligned}
$$

对内层积分，令

$$
 u=1+x^2+y^4,
\qquad du=4y^3\,dy,
$$

则

$$
\begin{aligned}
 \int_{\sqrt{x}}^{+\infty}
\frac{y^3}{(1+x^2+y^4)^2}\,dy
&=\frac14\int_{1+2x^2}^{+\infty}\frac{du}{u^2}\\
&=\frac{1}{4(1+2x^2)}.
\end{aligned}
$$

所以

$$
\begin{aligned}
 I
&=\frac14\int_0^{+\infty}\frac{dx}{1+2x^2}\\
&=\frac{1}{4\sqrt2}
\left.\arctan(\sqrt2x)\right|_0^{+\infty}\\
&=\frac{\pi}{8\sqrt2}.
\end{aligned}
$$
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

### （三）交换积分次序


<ExamQuestion>

1. **【1988-2-6 分】** 计算

$$

\int_1^2dx\int_{\sqrt{x}}^x
\sin\frac{\pi x}{2y}\,dy
+
\int_2^4dx\int_{\sqrt{x}}^2
\sin\frac{\pi x}{2y}\,dy.
$$

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>$ \frac{4}{\pi^2}+\frac{8}{\pi^3}$。</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
原积分区域由

$$
 y=\sqrt{x},\qquad y=x,
\qquad 1\le y\le2
$$

围成。将 $y=\sqrt{x}$ 改写为 $x=y^2$，交换积分次序后，区域统一表示为

$$
 1\le y\le2,
\qquad y\le x\le y^2.
$$

因此原式化为

$$
\begin{aligned}
 I
&=\int_1^2dy\int_y^{y^2}
\sin\frac{\pi x}{2y}\,dx.
\end{aligned}
$$

先对 $x$ 积分：

$$
\begin{aligned}
 \int_y^{y^2}
\sin\frac{\pi x}{2y}\,dx
&=\left.-\frac{2y}{\pi}
\cos\frac{\pi x}{2y}\right|_y^{y^2}\\
&=-\frac{2y}{\pi}\cos\frac{\pi y}{2},
\end{aligned}
$$

其中 $ \cos\frac\pi2=0$。故

$$
\begin{aligned}
 I
&=-\frac2\pi\int_1^2y\cos\frac{\pi y}{2}\,dy\\
&=-\frac2\pi
\left[
\frac{2y}{\pi}\sin\frac{\pi y}{2}
+\frac4{\pi^2}\cos\frac{\pi y}{2}
\right]_1^2\\
&=-\frac2\pi\left(-\frac4{\pi^2}-\frac2\pi\right)\\
&=\frac4{\pi^2}+\frac8{\pi^3}.
\end{aligned}
$$
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

2. **【1992-2-6 分】** 计算

$$

\int_{1/4}^{1/2}dy\int_{1/2}^{\sqrt y}e^{y/x}\,dx
+
\int_{1/2}^1dy\int_y^{\sqrt y}e^{y/x}\,dx.
$$

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>$ \frac{3e}{8}-\frac{\sqrt e}{2}$。</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
原区域由

$$
 y=x^2,
\qquad y=x,
\qquad \frac12\le x\le1
$$

围成。交换积分次序后可统一写成

$$
 \frac12\le x\le1,
\qquad x^2\le y\le x.
$$

于是

$$
\begin{aligned}
 I
&=\int_{1/2}^1dx\int_{x^2}^{x}e^{y/x}\,dy\\
&=\int_{1/2}^1x
\left.e^{y/x}\right|_{x^2}^{x}\,dx\\
&=\int_{1/2}^1x(e-e^x)\,dx.
\end{aligned}
$$

分别计算得

$$
 e\int_{1/2}^1x\,dx
=e\left[\frac{x^2}{2}\right]_{1/2}^1
=\frac{3e}{8},
$$

以及

$$
 \int_{1/2}^1xe^x\,dx
=\left[(x-1)e^x\right]_{1/2}^1
=\frac{\sqrt e}{2}.
$$

故

$$
 I=\frac{3e}{8}-\frac{\sqrt e}{2}.
$$
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

### （四）极坐标法


<ExamQuestion>

1. **【1994-4-6 分】** 设

$$
 D=\{(x,y)\mid x^2+y^2\le x+y+1\},
$$

计算

$$
 \iint_D(x+y)\,dx\,dy.
$$

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>$ \frac{3\pi}{2}$。</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
将区域方程配方：

$$
\begin{aligned}
 x^2+y^2\le x+y+1
&\Longleftrightarrow
\left(x-\frac12\right)^2
+\left(y-\frac12\right)^2
\le\frac32.
\end{aligned}
$$

因此 $D$ 是圆心为 $ \left(\frac12,\frac12\right)$、半径为 $ \sqrt{\frac32}$ 的圆域。

令

$$
 u=x-\frac12,
\qquad v=y-\frac12,
$$

则区域变为关于原点对称的圆域

$$
 u^2+v^2\le\frac32,
$$

且

$$
 x+y=u+v+1.
$$

由对称性，$u$ 与 $v$ 在圆域上的积分均为零，故

$$
\begin{aligned}
 \iint_D(x+y)\,dx\,dy
&=\iint_{u^2+v^2\le3/2}(u+v+1)\,du\,dv\\
&=\iint_{u^2+v^2\le3/2}1\,du\,dv\\
&=\pi\left(\sqrt{\frac32}\right)^2\\
&=\frac{3\pi}{2}.
\end{aligned}
$$
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

2. **【1996-2-6 分】** 设

$$
 D=\{(x,y)\mid0\le y\le x,
\ x^2+y^2\le2x\},
$$

计算

$$
 \iint_D\sqrt{x^2+y^2}\,dx\,dy.
$$

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>$ \frac{10\sqrt2}{9}$。</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
采用极坐标

$$
 x=r\cos\theta,
\qquad y=r\sin\theta,
\qquad dx\,dy=r\,dr\,d\theta.
$$

条件 $0\le y\le x$ 给出

$$
 0\le\theta\le\frac\pi4.
$$

圆域条件为

$$
 r^2\le2r\cos\theta,
$$

故

$$
 0\le r\le2\cos\theta.
$$

又 $ \sqrt{x^2+y^2}=r$，所以

$$
\begin{aligned}
 I
&=\int_0^{\pi/4}d\theta
\int_0^{2\cos\theta}r^2\,dr\\
&=\frac83\int_0^{\pi/4}\cos^3\theta\,d\theta\\
&=\frac83
\left[\sin\theta-\frac{\sin^3\theta}{3}\right]_0^{\pi/4}\\
&=\frac83\left(\frac{\sqrt2}{2}-\frac{\sqrt2}{12}\right)\\
&=\frac{10\sqrt2}{9}.
\end{aligned}
$$
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

3. **【1998-34-6 分】** 设

$$
 D=\{(x,y)\mid x^2+y^2\le x\},
$$

计算

$$
 \iint_D\sqrt{x}\,dx\,dy.
$$

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>$ \frac{8}{15}$。</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
区域边界

$$
 x^2+y^2=x
$$

是圆

$$
 \left(x-\frac12\right)^2+y^2=\frac14.
$$

该圆域位于右半平面，适合采用极坐标。令

$$
 x=r\cos\theta,
\qquad y=r\sin\theta,
\qquad dx\,dy=r\,dr\,d\theta.
$$

由

$$
 r^2\le r\cos\theta
$$

得

$$
 -\frac\pi2\le\theta\le\frac\pi2,
\qquad 0\le r\le\cos\theta.
$$

又因区域内 $x\ge0$，所以 $\sqrt{x}=\sqrt{r\cos\theta}$。于是

$$
\begin{aligned}
 I
&=\int_{-\pi/2}^{\pi/2}d\theta
\int_0^{\cos\theta}
\sqrt{r\cos\theta}\,r\,dr\\
&=\int_{-\pi/2}^{\pi/2}\sqrt{\cos\theta}
\left[\frac25r^{5/2}\right]_0^{\cos\theta}d\theta\\
&=\frac25\int_{-\pi/2}^{\pi/2}\cos^3\theta\,d\theta\\
&=\frac45\int_0^{\pi/2}\cos^3\theta\,d\theta\\
&=\frac45\cdot\frac23\\
&=\frac{8}{15}.
\end{aligned}
$$
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

4. **【1999-34-7 分】** 设 $D$ 是由

$$
 x=-2,
\qquad y=0,
\qquad y=2,
\qquad x=-\sqrt{2y-y^2}
$$

围成的平面区域，计算

$$
 \iint_D y\,dx\,dy.
$$

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>$ 4-\frac\pi2$。</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
按照先对 $x$ 积分的次序，区域可写成

$$
 0\le y\le2,
\qquad -2\le x\le-\sqrt{2y-y^2}.
$$

因此

$$
\begin{aligned}
 I
&=\int_0^2dy\int_{-2}^{-\sqrt{2y-y^2}}y\,dx\\
&=\int_0^2y\left(2-\sqrt{2y-y^2}\right)dy\\
&=2\int_0^2y\,dy-
\int_0^2y\sqrt{2y-y^2}\,dy.
\end{aligned}
$$

第一项为

$$
 2\int_0^2y\,dy=4.
$$

对于第二项，令 $t=y-1$，则

$$
 2y-y^2=1-t^2,
\qquad y=t+1,
$$

故

$$
\begin{aligned}
 \int_0^2y\sqrt{2y-y^2}\,dy
&=\int_{-1}^{1}(t+1)\sqrt{1-t^2}\,dt\\
&=\int_{-1}^{1}t\sqrt{1-t^2}\,dt
+\int_{-1}^{1}\sqrt{1-t^2}\,dt.
\end{aligned}
$$

其中第一项的被积函数为奇函数，积分为零；第二项等于单位圆的上半圆面积，即

$$
 \int_{-1}^{1}\sqrt{1-t^2}\,dt=\frac\pi2.
$$

所以

$$
 I=4-\frac\pi2.
$$
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

5. **【2000-3-6 分】** 设 $a>0$，区域 $D$ 由曲线

$$
 y=-a+\sqrt{a^2-x^2}
$$

与直线 $y=-x$ 围成，计算

$$
 \iint_D
\frac{\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}{\sqrt{4a^2-x^2-y^2}}\,d\sigma.
$$

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>$ a^2\left(\frac{\pi^2}{16}-\frac12\right)$。</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
曲线

$$
 y=-a+\sqrt{a^2-x^2}
$$

可化为

$$
 x^2+(y+a)^2=a^2,
$$

其极坐标方程为

$$
 r^2+2ar\sin\theta=0,
$$

即

$$
 r=-2a\sin\theta.
$$

直线 $y=-x$ 对应

$$
 \theta=-\frac\pi4.
$$

由区域位置可知

$$
 -\frac\pi4\le\theta\le0,
\qquad 0\le r\le-2a\sin\theta.
$$

又

$$
 \sqrt{x^2+y^2}=r,
\qquad d\sigma=r\,dr\,d\theta,
$$

因此

$$
\begin{aligned}
 I
&=\int_{-\pi/4}^{0}d\theta
\int_0^{-2a\sin\theta}
\frac{r^2}{\sqrt{4a^2-r^2}}\,dr.
\end{aligned}
$$

令 $\varphi=-\theta$，则

$$
 I=\int_0^{\pi/4}d\varphi
\int_0^{2a\sin\varphi}
\frac{r^2}{\sqrt{4a^2-r^2}}\,dr.
$$

在内层积分中令

$$
 r=2a\sin u,
\qquad 0\le u\le\varphi,
$$

则

$$
\begin{aligned}
 \int_0^{2a\sin\varphi}
\frac{r^2}{\sqrt{4a^2-r^2}}\,dr
&=4a^2\int_0^\varphi\sin^2u\,du\\
&=a^2(2\varphi-\sin2\varphi).
\end{aligned}
$$

于是

$$
\begin{aligned}
 I
&=a^2\int_0^{\pi/4}(2\varphi-\sin2\varphi)\,d\varphi\\
&=a^2\left[\varphi^2+\frac12\cos2\varphi\right]_0^{\pi/4}\\
&=a^2\left(\frac{\pi^2}{16}-\frac12\right).
\end{aligned}
$$
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

$$
 D=\{(x,y)\mid x^2+y^2\le y,\ x\ge0\},
$$

且

$$
 f(x,y)=\sqrt{1-x^2-y^2}
-\frac8\pi\iint_D f(u,v)\,du\,dv.
$$

求 $f(x,y)$。

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>
$$
 f(x,y)=\sqrt{1-x^2-y^2}
-\frac23+\frac{8}{9\pi}.
$$
</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
记

$$
 C=\iint_D f(u,v)\,du\,dv.
$$

则

$$
 f(x,y)=\sqrt{1-x^2-y^2}-\frac8\pi C.
$$

区域条件在极坐标下为

$$
 r^2\le r\sin\theta,
\qquad x\ge0,
$$

故

$$
 0\le\theta\le\frac\pi2,
\qquad 0\le r\le\sin\theta.
$$

区域 $D$ 是圆

$$
 x^2+\left(y-\frac12\right)^2\le\frac14
$$

的右半部分，因此

$$
 S_D=\frac12\pi\left(\frac12\right)^2
=\frac\pi8.
$$

对函数等式在 $D$ 上积分，得

$$
 C=A-\frac8\pi C\,S_D,
$$

其中

$$
 A=\iint_D\sqrt{1-x^2-y^2}\,dx\,dy.
$$

由于 $ \frac8\pi S_D=1$，所以

$$
 2C=A.
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
 A
&=\int_0^{\pi/2}d\theta
\int_0^{\sin\theta}\sqrt{1-r^2}\,r\,dr\\
&=\frac13\int_0^{\pi/2}
\left[1-(1-\sin^2\theta)^{3/2}\right]d\theta\\
&=\frac13\int_0^{\pi/2}(1-\cos^3\theta)\,d\theta\\
&=\frac13\left(\frac\pi2-\frac23\right)\\
&=\frac\pi6-\frac29.
\end{aligned}
$$

故

$$
 C=\frac A2
=\frac\pi{12}-\frac19.
$$

于是

$$
\begin{aligned}
 f(x,y)
&=\sqrt{1-x^2-y^2}
-\frac8\pi\left(\frac\pi{12}-\frac19\right)\\
&=\sqrt{1-x^2-y^2}
-\frac23+\frac{8}{9\pi}.
\end{aligned}
$$
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

9. **【2005-1-11 分】** 设

 p=\frac{v-u}{\sqrt2},

 p^2+q^2\le2,

 I

$$
 \int_0^1\sqrt{1+t^2}\,dt
=\frac12\left[\sqrt2+\ln(1+\sqrt2)\right],

且

 \int_1^2x\,dx=\frac32.
$$

因此

\boxed{ I=\frac34\left[\sqrt2+\ln(1+\sqrt2)\right]}.

</ExamExplanation>
