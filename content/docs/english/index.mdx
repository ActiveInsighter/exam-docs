<ExamArticle title="The value of paying attention" titleTranslation="注意力的价值" source="原创模拟文章 · 非历年真题">

<Paragraph>
<p>On an ordinary Tuesday, a group of volunteers walked through a small city park. Some listened to a podcast, while others were asked to put away their phones and notice the sounds, colours, and shapes around them. At the end of the walk, the second group felt calmer and remembered more of what they had seen. The difference surprised several volunteers, who had expected a familiar park to offer little worth noticing.</p>
<Translation>在一个普通的周二，一群志愿者穿过城市里的一座小公园。其中一些人边走边听播客，另一些人则被要求收起手机，留意周围的声音、色彩和形状。散步结束时，第二组人感觉更平静，也记住了更多看到的东西。这一差异让好几位志愿者感到意外——他们原以为一座熟悉的公园没什么值得留意的。</Translation>
</Paragraph>

<Paragraph>
<p>The following week, the volunteers returned to the park. They visited the same place, but this time they exchanged activities: those who had listened to a podcast now walked without headphones, and the others did the opposite. Once again, the people who observed their surroundings recalled more details. <Evidence for="english-reading-001">Because both groups experienced both activities in the same setting, the comparison helped the researchers separate the effect of attention from the appeal of a particular location.</Evidence></p>
<Translation>第二周，志愿者们回到了这座公园。他们去的是同一个地方，但这一次互换了活动：上次听播客的人这次不戴耳机散步，另一组则正好相反。结果再一次显示，观察周围环境的人记住了更多细节。由于两组人都在同一环境中体验了两种活动，这一对比帮助研究人员把注意力的作用与某个特定地点的吸引力区分开来。</Translation>
</Paragraph>

<Paragraph>
<p><Evidence for="english-reading-002">The researchers concluded that it was the way people directed their attention, rather than simply being outdoors, that made the difference.</Evidence> A tree they had passed a hundred times became interesting again; <Evidence for="english-reading-003">a bird call that had once disappeared into background noise became distinct</Evidence>. <Evidence for="english-reading-004">This does not mean that podcasts are harmful, or that every walk should become a formal exercise. It suggests, instead, that constantly filling our attention can leave little room for experiences already within reach.</Evidence></p>
<Translation>研究人员得出结论：带来差异的是人们分配注意力的方式，而不只是身处户外。一棵他们经过上百次的树重新变得有趣；一声曾经淹没在背景噪音里的鸟鸣变得清晰可辨。这并不意味着播客有害，也不意味着每一次散步都要变成一项正式的练习。相反，它说明：不停地填满我们的注意力，可能会让我们无暇感受那些本就触手可及的体验。</Translation>
</Paragraph>

<Paragraph>
<p>We often imagine that a memorable experience requires a distant journey or an expensive purchase. Yet the park experiment points to a more modest possibility: <Evidence for="english-reading-005">we can find something new in familiar surroundings by looking at them with fresh attention</Evidence>. The ordinary need not remain invisible just because it is ordinary. Before searching for a different world, we might begin by making a little more space to notice the one we already inhabit.</p>
<Translation>我们常常以为，难忘的经历需要一次远行或一笔昂贵的消费。然而，公园实验指向一种更朴素的可能：只要用全新的注意力去看待熟悉的环境，我们就能从中发现新意。平凡的事物不必因为平凡就一直被视而不见。在寻找另一个世界之前，我们或许可以先多留出一点空间，去留意自己已身处其中的这个世界。</Translation>
</Paragraph>

<Question id="english-reading-001" type="single">

<p lang="en">Why did the volunteers exchange activities during their second visit?</p>

<Option>To discover which part of the park was most attractive.</Option>
<Option correct>To distinguish the effect of attention from that of the setting.</Option>
<Option>To test whether a new podcast could improve their memory.</Option>
<Option>To encourage them to visit unfamiliar places.</Option>

<Solution>

定位第二段末句。separate the effect of attention from… 对应选项中的 distinguish…from…。交换活动让两组都体验两种方式，同时控制地点，是为了判断差异是否来自注意力。

- A：原文保持地点不变，没有比较公园的不同区域。
- C：实验并未更换或评估播客内容。
- D：same setting 表明两次都在同一个熟悉的地点。

</Solution>

</Question>

<Question id="english-reading-002" type="single">

<p lang="en">What can we infer from the results of the two walks?</p>

<Option>A change of scenery is necessary for a memorable experience.</Option>
<Option>Listening to a podcast always makes people anxious.</Option>
<Option correct>Paying attention to nearby details can enrich an ordinary experience.</Option>
<Option>People remember places better when they walk alone.</Option>

<Solution>

第一段给出初次结果，第三段概括原因：决定差异的是注意力的分配方式。由此可推知，留意身边细节可以改善平常的体验，而不需要改变地点。注意 always、necessary 等绝对表述。

- A：地点未变，体验却发生了变化；necessary 扩大了结论。
- B：原文没有证明焦虑，也明确反对将播客视为有害。
- D：独自散步不是实验比较的变量。

</Solution>

</Question>

<Question id="english-reading-003" type="single">

<p lang="en">The word “distinct” in Paragraph 3 is closest in meaning to _____.</p>

<Option correct>clearly noticeable</Option>
<Option>completely unfamiliar</Option>
<Option>unusually unpleasant</Option>
<Option>physically distant</Option>

<Solution>

根据同一句的对照猜词：过去鸟鸣 disappeared into background noise，如今 became distinct。变化在于从被忽略到可辨认，因此 distinct 表示「清楚可察觉的」。

- B：声音并不是新的，只是过去没有被留意。
- C：上下文没有负面的情绪评价。
- D：这里讨论感知的清晰程度，而非距离。

</Solution>

</Question>

<Question id="english-reading-004" type="single">

<p lang="en">Which statement best describes the author’s view of podcasts?</p>

<Option>They should be avoided whenever people go outdoors.</Option>
<Option>They are the main cause of poor memory.</Option>
<Option>They are more valuable than direct observation.</Option>
<Option correct>They are not necessarily harmful, but constant distraction has a cost.</Option>

<Solution>

作者先用 This does not mean… 限定结论，随后用 instead 表明真正关注的问题：持续占满注意力可能压缩感受周围世界的空间。D 同时保留了这个让步与提醒。

- A：作者未要求户外活动时禁止播客。
- B：原文没有将记忆问题归因于播客。
- C：原文并未作出这种价值排序。

</Solution>

</Question>

<Question id="english-reading-005" type="single">

<p lang="en">Which of the following is the best title for the passage?</p>

<Option>The hidden dangers of digital entertainment</Option>

<Option>How to plan the perfect journey</Option>
<Option>Why city parks need more visitors</Option>

<Solution>

主旨题要同时看实验结论和结尾建议。第三段指出 attention 是关键，第四段把它推广为用 fresh attention 发现 familiar surroundings 中的新意。B 概括全文，其余标题只抓住局部词汇。

- A：文章不是对数字娱乐的批判，这只涉及局部内容。
- C：旅行只是最后一段的对照，不是全文论述对象。
- D：公园是实验场景，文章没有讨论游客数量。

</Solution>
