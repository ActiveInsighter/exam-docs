<ExamQuestion>

1. （04—1 4分）欧拉方程 $x^2 \frac{\mathrm{d}^2 y}{\mathrm{d}x^2} + 4x \frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} + 2y = 0 (x>0)$ 的通解为 _________．

<ExamAnswer>$y=\frac{C_1}{x}+\frac{C_2}{x^2}$</ExamAnswer>

<ExamSolution>
