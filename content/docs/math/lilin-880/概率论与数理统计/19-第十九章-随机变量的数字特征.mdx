# 第十九章 随机变量的数字特征

## 本章方法速记

- 数学期望优先用线性性：$E(aX+bY+c)=aEX+bEY+c$；独立不是期望线性性的前提。
- 方差与协方差是综合题核心：$D(X\pm Y)=DX+DY\pm2\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)$，$\operatorname{Cov}(aX+b,cY+d)=ac\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)$。
- 独立且二阶矩存在 $\Rightarrow$ 不相关；反之一般不成立。二维正态情形下，不相关与独立等价。
- $|\rho_{XY}|=1$ 表示两个随机变量几乎处处存在线性关系；符号由线性关系斜率决定。
- 最值、次序统计量常先求分布函数；计数型随机变量优先识别二项分布或用示性变量拆分期望、方差。

## 基础题

### 一、选择题

<ExamQuestion>

1. 设随机变量 $X$ 服从参数为 $2$ 的指数分布，则 $Y = 2X + \mathrm{e}^{-2X}$ 的期望 $EY = (\quad)$.

<ExamChoices>
<ExamOption>A. $\frac{3}{2}$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>B. $\frac{2}{3}$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>C. $\frac{3}{4}$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>D. $\frac{4}{3}$</ExamOption>
</ExamChoices>

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>A</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
由于 $X$ 服从参数为 $2$ 的指数分布, 故 $EX = \frac{1}{2}$. 又
$$E(e^{-2X}) = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} e^{-2x} f(x) dx = \int_{0}^{+\infty} e^{-2x} \cdot 2e^{-2x} dx$$
$$= 2 \int_{0}^{+\infty} e^{-4x} dx = \frac{1}{2},$$
所以
$$EY = 2EX + E(e^{-2X}) = 2 \times \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{2}.$$
选项 A 正确.
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

<ExamQuestion>

2. 设随机变量 $X \sim B(n, p)$，且 $EX = 2.4, DX = 1.44$，则 $(\quad)$.

<ExamChoices>
<ExamOption>A. $n = 8, p = 0.6$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>B. $n = 6, p = 0.4$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>C. $n = 4, p = 0.5$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>D. $n = 12, p = 0.1$</ExamOption>
</ExamChoices>

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>B</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
由 $X \sim B(n, p)$, 可知 $EX = np, DX = np(1-p)$, 故
$$\begin{cases} np = 2.4, \\ np(1-p) = 1.44, \end{cases}$$
解得 $n = 6, p = 0.4$. 故选 B.
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

<ExamQuestion>

3. 设 $EX$ 与 $E(X^2)$ 均存在，则 $(\quad)$.

<ExamChoices>
<ExamOption>A. $E(X^2) \geqslant (EX)^2$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>B. $E(X^2) < (EX)^2$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>C. $E(X^2) < EX$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>D. $E(X^2) > EX$</ExamOption>
</ExamChoices>

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>A</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
由 $DX = E(X^2) - (EX)^2 \ge 0$, 知 $E(X^2) \ge (EX)^2$, 故选 A.
选项 C 与 D 均不一定成立, 如 $X \sim N\left(\frac{1}{2}, \frac{1}{4}\right)$,
$$EX = \frac{1}{2}, DX = \frac{1}{4},$$
$$E(X^2) = DX + (EX)^2 = \frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{4} = \frac{1}{2},$$
则 $E(X^2) = EX = \frac{1}{2}$.
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

<ExamQuestion>

4. 设 $(X, Y)$ 服从二维正态分布，则 $U = X + Y$ 与 $V = X - Y$ 不相关的充分必要条件是 $(\quad)$.

<ExamChoices>
<ExamOption>A. $EX = EY$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>B. $E(X^2) = E(Y^2)$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>C. $E(X^2) + (EY)^2 = E(Y^2) + (EX)^2$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>D. $E(X^2) + (EX)^2 = E(Y^2) + (EY)^2$</ExamOption>
</ExamChoices>

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>C</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
因为 $U$ 与 $V$ 不相关 $\iff \operatorname{Cov}(U, V) = 0$, 由于
$$\begin{aligned} \operatorname{Cov}(U, V) &= \operatorname{Cov}(X+Y, X-Y) \\ &= \operatorname{Cov}(X, X) - \operatorname{Cov}(X, Y) + \operatorname{Cov}(Y, X) - \operatorname{Cov}(Y, Y) \\ &= \operatorname{Cov}(X, X) - \operatorname{Cov}(Y, Y) = DX - DY = 0, \end{aligned}$$
故 $U, V$ 不相关的充分必要条件是 $DX = DY$.
而 $DX = E(X^2) - (EX)^2, DY = E(Y^2) - (EY)^2$,
故选 C.
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

<ExamQuestion>

5. 设随机变量 $X$ 与 $Y$ 相互独立，且均存在非零方差，$DX \neq DY$，则 $(\quad)$.

<ExamChoices>
<ExamOption>A. $2X + 1$ 与 $2Y - 1$ 相关</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>B. $X + Y$ 与 $X - Y$ 不相关</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>C. $\mathrm{e}^X$ 与 $Y + 1$ 相互独立</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>D. $X + Y$ 与 $Y + 1$ 相互独立</ExamOption>
</ExamChoices>

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>C</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
由 $X$ 与 $Y$ 相互独立, 知 $X$ 与 $Y$ 不相关.
由 $\operatorname{Cov}(2X+1, 2Y-1) = 4\operatorname{Cov}(X, Y) = 0$, 知 $2X+1$ 与 $2Y-1$ 不相关, 排除项 A.
由 $\operatorname{Cov}(X+Y, X-Y) = \operatorname{Cov}(X, X) - \operatorname{Cov}(Y, Y) = DX - DY \neq 0$, 知 $X+Y$ 与 $X-Y$ 相关, 排除项 B.
利用定义可验证 $e^X$ 与 $Y+1$ 相互独立.
当 $x > 0$ 时,

$$P\{e^X \leqslant x, Y+1 \leqslant y\} = P\{X \leqslant \ln x, Y \leqslant y-1\}$$
$$= P\{X \leqslant \ln x\} \cdot P\{Y \leqslant y-1\}$$
$$= P\{e^X \leqslant x\} \cdot P\{Y+1 \leqslant y\};$$

当 $x \leqslant 0$ 时, $P\{e^X \leqslant x\} = 0$, 所以有
$$P\{e^X \leqslant x, Y+1 \leqslant y\} = 0 = P\{e^X \leqslant x\} \cdot P\{Y+1 \leqslant y\},$$
故 $e^X$ 与 $Y+1$ 相互独立, 选项 C 正确.
由 $\operatorname{Cov}(X+Y, Y+1) = \operatorname{Cov}(X, Y) + \operatorname{Cov}(Y, Y) = DY \neq 0$, 知 $X+Y$ 与 $Y+1$ 相关, 从而 $X+Y$ 与 $Y+1$ 不相互独立, 排除选项 D.
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

<ExamQuestion>

6. 设 $X \sim N(0, 1), Y \sim N(1, 4)$，且 $\rho_{XY} = 1$，则 $(\quad)$.

<ExamChoices>
<ExamOption>A. $P\{Y = 2X + 1\} = 1$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>B. $P\{Y = -2X + 1\} = 1$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>C. $P\{Y = -2X - 1\} = 1$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>D. $P\{Y = 2X - 1\} = 1$</ExamOption>
</ExamChoices>

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>A</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
由 $\rho_{XY}=1$，可知存在 $a>0,b$，使 $P\{Y=aX+b\}=1$。同时
$$a=\frac{\sigma_Y}{\sigma_X}=\frac{2}{1}=2,$$
而 $EX=0,EY=1$，故 $b=EY-aEX=1$。因此
$$P\{Y=2X+1\}=1,$$
选 A。

**知识点与技巧：** 当随机变量方差均非零时，$|\rho_{XY}|=1$ 当且仅当二者几乎处处满足线性关系；$\rho=1$ 对应正斜率，$\rho=-1$ 对应负斜率。
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

### 二、填空题

<ExamQuestion>

1. 设随机变量 $X$ 的概率密度为 $f(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi}}\mathrm{e}^{-x^2+2x-1}(-\infty < x < +\infty)$，则 $E(X^2) = $ _________.

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>$\frac{3}{2}$</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
$$f(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi}} e^{-x^2+2x-1} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi} \cdot \sqrt{2}} e^{-\frac{(x-1)^2}{2 \cdot \frac{1}{2}}},$$
知 $X \sim N\left(1, \frac{1}{2}\right)$, 故 $EX = 1, DX = \frac{1}{2}$, 所以
$$E(X^2) = DX + (EX)^2 = \frac{1}{2} + 1^2 = \frac{3}{2}.$$
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

<ExamQuestion>

2. 从 $1, 2, 3, 4, 5$ 中任取一数 $X$，再从 $1, \cdots, X$ 中任取一数 $Y$，则 $EY = $ _________.

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>$2$</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
先求 $Y$ 的分布律.
$Y$ 的所有可能取值为 $1, 2, 3, 4, 5$, 由全概率公式得
$$P\{Y = k\} = \sum_{i=k}^{5} P\{X = i\} \cdot P\{Y = k \mid X = i\} = \frac{1}{5} \sum_{i=k}^{5} \frac{1}{i} \quad (k = 1, 2, \cdots, 5),$$
故
$$EY = \sum_{k=1}^{5} k \cdot P\{Y = k\} = \sum_{k=1}^{5} \left(k \cdot \frac{1}{5} \sum_{i=k}^{5} \frac{1}{i}\right) = 2.$$
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

<ExamQuestion>

3. 设随机变量 $X$ 的分布函数 $F(x) = 0.3\Phi\left(\frac{x-4}{2}\right) + 0.7\Phi\left(\frac{x+1}{3}\right)$，其中 $\Phi(x)$ 为标准正态分布的分布函数，则 $EX = $ _________.

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>$0.5$</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
先求 $X$ 的概率密度, 再求 $EX$.
$$f(x) = F'(x) = 0.3 \varphi\left(\frac{x-4}{2}\right) \cdot \frac{1}{2} + 0.7 \varphi\left(\frac{x+1}{3}\right) \cdot \frac{1}{3},$$
其中 $\varphi(x)$ 为 $X \sim N(0, 1)$ 的概率密度.

$$EX = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} x f(x) \, dx = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} 0.3 \cdot \frac{1}{2} \cdot x \varphi\left(\frac{x - 4}{2}\right) \, dx + \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} 0.7 \cdot \frac{1}{3} \cdot x \varphi\left(\frac{x + 1}{3}\right) \, dx$$

$$= 0.3 \times \frac{1}{2} \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} x \varphi\left(\frac{x - 4}{2}\right) \, dx + 0.7 \times \frac{1}{3} \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} x \varphi\left(\frac{x + 1}{3}\right) \, dx$$

$$= 0.3 \times \frac{1}{2} \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} (2t + 4) \varphi(t) \cdot 2dt + 0.7 \times \frac{1}{3} \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} (3t - 1) \varphi(t) \cdot 3dt$$

$$= 0.3 \times 4 + 0.7 \times (-1) = 0.5.$$

**知识点与技巧：** ① $\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} t \varphi(t) \, dt = \mu = 0$.

② 设 $X \sim N(\mu, \sigma^2)(\sigma > 0)$, 则分布函数

$$F(x) = P\{X \leqslant x\} = P\left\{\frac{X - \mu}{\sigma} \leqslant \frac{x - \mu}{\sigma}\right\} = \Phi\left(\frac{x - \mu}{\sigma}\right),$$

即分布函数为 $\Phi\left(\frac{x - \mu}{\sigma}\right)$, 相应随机变量 $X$ 有 $EX = \mu$.

③ 一般结论: 若

$$F(x) = k_1 \Phi\left(\frac{x - \mu_1}{\sigma_1}\right) + k_2 \Phi\left(\frac{x - \mu_2}{\sigma_2}\right),$$

当 $k_1 + k_2 = 1$ 时, 有 $EX = k_1 \mu_1 + k_2 \mu_2$.
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

<ExamQuestion>

4. 设 $(X, Y) \sim N(\mu_1, \mu_2, \sigma^2, \sigma^2; \rho)$，若 $\frac{3X+Y}{2}$ 与 $\frac{X-2Y}{3}$ 不相关，则相关系数 $\rho = $ _________.

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>$\frac{1}{5}$</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
由于

$$\operatorname{Cov}\left(\frac{3X + Y}{2}, \frac{X - 2Y}{3}\right)$$

$$= \frac{1}{6} \operatorname{Cov}(3X + Y, X - 2Y)$$

$$= \frac{1}{6} [3\operatorname{Cov}(X, X) + \operatorname{Cov}(Y, X) - 6\operatorname{Cov}(X, Y) - 2\operatorname{Cov}(Y, Y)]$$

$$= \frac{1}{6} [3DX - 5\operatorname{Cov}(X, Y) - 2DY]$$

$$= \frac{1}{6} (3\sigma^2 - 5\rho\sigma^2 - 2\sigma^2) = \frac{1}{6}\sigma^2 (1 - 5\rho) = 0,$$

故 $\rho = \frac{1}{5}$.
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

<ExamQuestion>

5. 设二维随机变量 $(X, Y)$ 的概率密度为

$$
f(x, y) = \begin{cases}
xe^{-x(1+y)}, & x > 0, y > 0, \\
0, & \text{其他},
\end{cases}
$$

则 $P\{X > 2 \mid X > EX\} = $ _________.

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>$e^{-1}$</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
先求 $X$ 的边缘概率密度.

当 $x \leqslant 0$ 时, $f_X(x) = 0$.

当 $x > 0$ 时, $f_X(x) = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} f(x, y) \, dy = \int_{0}^{+\infty} x e^{-x(1+y)} \, dy = e^{-x}$.

故 $$f_X(x) = \begin{cases} e^{-x}, & x > 0, \\ 0, & x \leqslant 0, \end{cases}$$ 即 $X \sim E(1)$, 所以 $EX = \frac{1}{\lambda} = 1$.

由指数分布的无记忆性, 有

$$P\{X > 2 \mid X > EX\} = P\{X > 2 \mid X > 1\} = P\{X > 1 + 1 \mid X > 1\}$$

$$= P\{X > 1\} = 1 - P\{X \leqslant 1\}$$

$$= 1 - (1 - e^{-1}) = e^{-1}.$$
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

### 三、解答题

<ExamQuestion>

1. 设 $X$ 与 $Y$ 的概率分布分别为

| $X$ | $0$ | $1$ |
| :---: | :---: | :---: |
| $p$ | $\frac{1}{3}$ | $\frac{2}{3}$ |

| $Y$ | $-1$ | $0$ | $1$ |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| $p$ | $\frac{1}{3}$ | $\frac{1}{3}$ | $\frac{1}{3}$ |

且 $P\{X^2 = Y^2\} = 1$。求：

$(I)$ $(X, Y)$ 的概率分布；

$(II)$ $\rho_{XY}$.

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>见解析</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
(I) 由 $P\{X^2 = Y^2\} = 1$, 知 $P\{X^2 \neq Y^2\} = 0$, 故

$$P\{X = 0, Y = -1\} = P\{X = 0, Y = 1\} = P\{X = 1, Y = 0\} = 0.$$

又

$$P\{X = 1, Y = -1\} = P\{X = 0, Y = 0\} = P\{X = 1, Y = 1\} = \frac{1}{3},$$

故 $(X, Y)$ 的联合概率分布为

| $Y \backslash X$ | $0$ | $1$ |
| :---: | :---: | :---: |
| $-1$ | $0$ | $\frac{1}{3}$ |
| $0$ | $\frac{1}{3}$ | $0$ |
| $1$ | $0$ | $\frac{1}{3}$ |

(Ⅱ)由(Ⅰ)知, $XY$ 的概率分布为

| $XY$ | $-1$ | $0$ | $1$ |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| $p$ | $\frac{1}{3}$ | $\frac{1}{3}$ | $\frac{1}{3}$ |

故 $E(XY) = 0$.

又 $EX = \frac{2}{3}, EY = 0$, 故 $\operatorname{Cov}(X, Y) = E(XY) - EX \cdot EY = 0$, 所以 $\rho_{XY} = 0$.
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

<ExamQuestion>

2. 设随机变量 $X, Y, Z$，满足 $EX = EY = 1, EZ = -1, DX = DY = DZ = 1, \rho_{XY} = 0, \rho_{XZ} = \frac{1}{2}$，
$\rho_{YZ} = -\frac{1}{2}$，求 $E(X + Y - 2Z), D(X + Y + Z)$。

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>见解析</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
$E(X + Y - 2Z) = EX + EY - 2EZ = 1 + 1 - 2 \times (-1) = 4$,

$D(X + Y + Z) = DX + DY + DZ + 2\operatorname{Cov}(X, Y) + 2\operatorname{Cov}(X, Z) + 2\operatorname{Cov}(Y, Z)$.

$$\operatorname{Cov}(X, Y) = \sqrt{DX} \cdot \sqrt{DY} \cdot \rho_{XY} = 1 \times 0 = 0,$$

$$\operatorname{Cov}(X, Z) = \sqrt{DX} \cdot \sqrt{DZ} \cdot \rho_{XZ} = 1 \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{2},$$

$$\operatorname{Cov}(Y, Z) = \sqrt{DY} \cdot \sqrt{DZ} \cdot \rho_{YZ} = 1 \times \left(-\frac{1}{2}\right) = -\frac{1}{2},$$

故 $D(X + Y + Z) = 1 + 1 + 1 + 0 + 2 \times \frac{1}{2} + 2 \times \left(-\frac{1}{2}\right) = 3$.
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

<ExamQuestion>

3. 设 $X$ 与 $Y$ 相互独立，且均服从 $N\left(1, \frac{1}{2}\right)$，求 $D(|X - Y|)$。

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>见解析</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
由 $X, Y$ 相互独立, 可知
$$Z = X - Y \sim N\left(1 - 1, \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2}\right),$$
即 $Z \sim N(0, 1)$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
E(|Z|) &= \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} |z| \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-\frac{z^2}{2}} dz = 2 \int_{0}^{+\infty} \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} ze^{-\frac{z^2}{2}} dz \\
&= \frac{\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{\pi}} \left[ 2 \int_{0}^{+\infty} \left(\frac{z}{\sqrt{2}}\right)^{2-1} \cdot e^{-\left(\frac{z}{\sqrt{2}}\right)^2} d\left(\frac{z}{\sqrt{2}}\right) \right] \\
&= \frac{\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{\pi}} \Gamma(1) = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{\pi}},
\end{aligned}
$$

$E(|Z|^2) = E(Z^2) = DZ + (EZ)^2 = 1 + 0 = 1$,

故
$$D(|X - Y|) = D(|Z|) = E(|Z|^2) - [E(|Z|)]^2 = 1 - \frac{2}{\pi}.$$

**知识点与技巧：** ① 计算 $\int_{0}^{+\infty} \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} ze^{-\frac{z^2}{2}} dz$ 可以凑微分, 也可用 $\Gamma$ 函数:
$$\Gamma(\alpha) = \int_{0}^{+\infty} x^{\alpha-1} \cdot e^{-x} dx \stackrel{令 x = y^2}{=} 2 \int_{0}^{+\infty} y^{2\alpha-1} e^{-y^2} dy,$$
$$\Gamma(1) = 1, \Gamma\left(\frac{1}{2}\right) = \sqrt{\pi},$$
$$\Gamma(\alpha + 1) = \alpha\Gamma(\alpha), \Gamma(n + 1) = n! \quad (n \text{ 为正整数}).$$
② 本题若用二维随机变量函数的期望公式
$$E(|X - Y|) = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} |x - y| f_X(x) f_Y(y) dx dy,$$
计算量较大.
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

<ExamQuestion>

4. 设 $X$ 的概率密度为 $f(x) = \frac{1}{2}\mathrm{e}^{-|x|}, -\infty < x < +\infty$。
(I) 求 $EX$ 和 $DX$;
(II) 求 $\operatorname{Cov}(X, |X|)$，并判别 $X$ 与 $|X|$ 是否不相关;
(III) 问 $X$ 与 $|X|$ 是否相互独立?

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>见解析</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
(I) $EX = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} x f(x) dx = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} x \cdot \frac{1}{2} e^{-|x|} dx = 0$ (奇函数),
$$\begin{aligned}
DX &= E(X^2) - (EX)^2 = E(X^2) \\
&= \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} x^2 f(x) dx = 2 \int_{0}^{+\infty} x^2 \cdot \frac{1}{2} e^{-x} dx = 2.
\end{aligned}$$

(II) $\operatorname{Cov}(X, |X|) = E\!\left[X|X|\right] - EX \cdot E(|X|) = E\!\left[X|X|\right]$.
而 $\quad E\!\left[X|X|\right] = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} x \cdot |x| f(x) dx = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} x \cdot |x| \cdot \frac{1}{2} e^{-|x|} dx = 0,$
故 $\operatorname{Cov}(X, |X|) = 0$, 即 $X$ 与 $|X|$ 不相关.
(III) 对任意给定的 $0 < a < +\infty$, 显然事件 $\{|X| \leqslant a\}$ 包含于事件 $\{X \leqslant a\}$, 且
$$P\{X \leqslant a\} < 1, P\{|X| \leqslant a\} > 0,$$
故 $\quad P\{X \leqslant a, |X| \leqslant a\} = P\{|X| \leqslant a\}.$
但 $\quad P\{X \leqslant a\} \cdot P\{|X| \leqslant a\} < P\{|X| \leqslant a\},$
从而 $\quad P\{X \leqslant a, |X| \leqslant a\} \neq P\{X \leqslant a\} \cdot P\{|X| \leqslant a\},$
因此, $X$ 与 $|X|$ 不相互独立.

**知识点与技巧：** ① 本题验证 $X$ 与 $|X|$ 不相互独立, 是用定义证明的. 若用两个随机变量 $X$ 与 $Y$ 的联合概率密度 $f(x, y)$ 是否等于 $f_X(x) \cdot f_Y(y)$ 来证明, 则会带来烦琐的计算.
② 本题也可看出: $X, Y$ 不相关 $\not\Rightarrow X, Y$ 相互独立.
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>C</ExamAnswer>

DX \cdot DY &= [E(X^2) - (EX)^2][E(Y^2) - (EY)^2] \\

> \begin{aligned}
> D(XY) &= E[(XY)^2] - [E(XY)]^2 = E(X^2) \cdot E(Y^2) - (EX)^2 \cdot (EY)^2 \\
> &= [DX + (EX)^2][DY + (EY)^2] - (EX)^2 \cdot (EY)^2 \\
> &= DXDY + (EX)^2 DY + (EY)^2 DX.
> \end{aligned}
> $$
</ExamExplanation>

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>2</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
根据概率分布的性质, 有 $\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{C}{k!} = Ce = 1$, 可得 $C = e^{-1}$.

注意到 $X$ 服从泊松分布,
$$P\{X = k\} = \frac{\lambda^k}{k!}e^{-\lambda} (k = 0, 1, 2, \cdots),$$

对比 $P\{X = k\} = \frac{e^{-1}}{k!} (k = 0, 1, 2, \cdots)$, 知 $\lambda = 1$, 即 $X \sim P(1)$, 故
$$EX = DX = \lambda = 1,$$

$$E(X^2) = DX + (EX)^2 = 1 + 1^2 = 2.$$

2. 设随机变量 $X_1, X_2, \cdots, X_n$ 独立同分布，且有相同的概率密度，则概率 $P\{X_n>\min\{X_1, X_2, \cdots, X_{n-1}\}\}=$ ______ .

<ExamSolution>

<ExamExplanation>
由已知, 有 $X_n$ 与 $Y = \min\{X_1, X_2, \cdots, X_{n-1}\}$ 相互独立, 且 $X_1, X_2, \cdots, X_n$ 独立同分布, 记其分布函数分别为 $F_1(x), F_2(x), \cdots, F_n(x)$, 概率密度分别为 $f_1(x), f_2(x), \cdots, f_n(x)$, 则

$$
\begin{aligned}
F_Y(y) &= P\{Y \leqslant y\} = P\{\min\{X_1, X_2, \cdots, X_{n-1}\} \leqslant y\} \\
&= 1 - P\{X_1 > y\} \cdot P\{X_2 > y\} \cdots \cdots P\{X_{n-1} > y\} \\
&= 1 - [1 - F_1(y)]^{n-1},
\end{aligned}
$$

故
$$f_Y(y) = F_Y'(y) = (n-1)[1 - F_1(y)]^{n-2} \cdot f_1(y),$$
所以

$$
\begin{aligned}
P\{X_n > Y\} &= \iint_{x > y} f_n(x)(n-1)f_1(y)[1 - F_1(y)]^{n-2} \mathrm{d}x \mathrm{d}y \\

&= \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} \{1 - [1 - F_1(x)]^{n-1}\} f_n(x) \mathrm{d}x \\
&= 1 + \left. \frac{[1 - F_1(x)]^n}{n} \right|_{-\infty}^{+\infty} = 1 - \frac{1}{n}.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$E(Y_2) = P\{Y_2 = 1\} = 1 - P\{Y_2 = 0\}$$
$$= 1 - P\{X_2 - X_1 = 2\} = 1 - P\{X_1 = 0, X_2 = 2\} = 1 - p^2(1 - p).$$
$Y_1 Y_2$ 也服从 $0-1$ 分布, 故
$$P\{Y_1 Y_2 = 0\} = P\{(Y_1 = 0) \cup (Y_2 = 0)\}$$
$$= P\{Y_1 = 0\} + P\{Y_2 = 0\} - P\{Y_1 = 0, Y_2 = 0\}$$
$$= 3p(1 - p)^2 + p^2(1 - p),$$
这里 $P\{Y_1 = 0, Y_2 = 0\} = P\{X_2 + X_1 = 1, X_2 - X_1 = 2\} = P\{\varnothing\} = 0$, 故

所以
$$\begin{aligned}
P\{Y_1Y_2=1\} &= 1-P\{Y_1Y_2=0\} = 1-3p(1-p)^2-p^2(1-p), \\
\operatorname{Cov}(Y_1,Y_2) &= E(Y_1Y_2)-E(Y_1)E(Y_2) \\
&= 0\cdot P\{Y_1Y_2=0\}+1\cdot P\{Y_1Y_2=1\}-[1-3p(1-p)^2]\cdot[1-p^2(1-p)] \\
&= -3p^3(1-p)^3 \overset{\text{记}}{=} f(p).
\end{aligned}$$

(II)由(I)知,$f(p)=-3p^3(1-p)^3$.令
$$f'(p) = -9p^2(1-p)^2\cdot(1-2p) = 0,$$

得 $p=0(\text{舍去}),p=1(\text{舍去}),p=\dfrac{1}{2}$。

当 $p<\dfrac{1}{2}$ 时,$f'(p)<0$;当 $p>\dfrac{1}{2}$ 时,$f'(p)>0$,故 $p=\dfrac{1}{2}$ 是 $f(p)$ 在 $(0,1)$ 内唯一的极小值点,

也是最小值点,所以当 $p=\dfrac{1}{2}$ 时,$\operatorname{Cov}(Y_1,Y_2)$ 取值最小。

**知识点与技巧：** 求 $P\{X_1+X_2=1\}$,利用 $X_1$ 与 $X_2$ 相互独立.
$X_1 \sim B(1,p), X_2 \sim B(2,p)$,
且 $X_1$ 与 $X_2$ 相互独立,则 $X_1+X_2 \sim B(1+2,p) = B(3,p)$.

6. 设随机变量 $X$ 与 $Y$ 相互独立，$X$ 的概率分布为 $P\{X=0\} = P\{X=1\} = \frac{1}{2}$，$Y$ 在 $[0, 1]$ 上服从均匀分布。求：

$(I) Z = X + Y$ 的分布函数与概率密度；

$(II)$ 相关系数 $\rho_{XZ}$.

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>见解析</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
(I)由已知,得 $Y$ 的概率密度为
$$f(y) = \begin{cases} 1, & 0\leqslant y \leqslant 1, \\ 0, & \text{其他}, \end{cases}$$

因此,$Y$ 的分布函数为
$$F(y) = \int_{-\infty}^{y} f(t)\,\mathrm{d}t = \begin{cases} 0, & y < 0, \\ y, & 0\leqslant y \leqslant 1, \\ 1, & y > 1. \end{cases}$$

令 $G(z)$ 是 $Z=X+Y$ 的分布函数,则当 $z<0$ 时,$G(z)=0$;当 $z\ge 2$ 时,$G(z)=1$;当 $0\leqslant z < 2$ 时,
$$\begin{aligned}
G(z) &= P\{Z\leqslant z\} = P\{X+Y\leqslant z\} \\
&= P\{X+Y\leqslant z, X=0\} + P\{X+Y\leqslant z, X=1\} \\
&= P\{X=0, Y\leqslant z\} + P\{X=1, Y\leqslant z-1\} \\
&= P\{X=0\}\cdot P\{Y\leqslant z\} + P\{X=1\}\cdot P\{Y\leqslant z-1\} \\
&= \dfrac{1}{2}P\{Y\leqslant z\} + \dfrac{1}{2}P\{Y\leqslant z-1\} \\
&= \begin{cases}
\dfrac{1}{2}z + 0, & 0\leqslant z < 1, \\
\dfrac{1}{2} + \dfrac{1}{2}(z-1), & 1\leqslant z < 2
\end{cases} \\
&= \dfrac{1}{2}z.
\end{aligned}$$

综上可得,$Z$ 的分布函数为
$$G(z) = \begin{cases} 0, & z<0, \\ \dfrac{z}{2}, & 0\leqslant z < 2, \\ 1, & z \ge 2, \end{cases}$$

$Z$ 的概率密度为
$$f_z(z) = G'(z) = \begin{cases} \dfrac{1}{2}, & 0\leqslant z < 2, \\ 0, & \text{其他}, \end{cases}$$

即服从 $U[0,2]$。

(Ⅱ) 由于
$$\rho_{XZ} = \frac{\operatorname{Cov}(X,Z)}{\sqrt{DX}\sqrt{DZ}} = \frac{E(XZ) - EX \cdot EZ}{\sqrt{DX}\sqrt{DZ}},$$
其中
$$\begin{aligned}
EX &= \frac{1}{2}, EY = \frac{0+1}{2} = \frac{1}{2}, EZ = \frac{0+2}{2} = 1, \\
DX &= p(1-p) = \frac{1}{2} \times \left(1 - \frac{1}{2}\right) = \frac{1}{4}, \\
DZ &= \frac{(2-0)^2}{12} = \frac{1}{3}, \\
E(XZ) &= E[X(X+Y)] = E(X^2) + E(XY) \\
&= \frac{1}{2} + EX \cdot EY = \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{4}, \\
\text{故 } \rho_{XZ} &= \frac{\frac{3}{4} - \frac{1}{2} \times 1}{\sqrt{\frac{1}{4}} \times \sqrt{\frac{1}{3}}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}.
\end{aligned}$$
</ExamExplanation>

<ExamSolution>

8. 设随机变量 $X_1$ 与 $X_2$ 相互独立，且均服从 $N(0,1)$，$X_3$ 的分布律为 $P\{X_3=-1\}=\frac{1}{4}$，$P\{X_3=1\}=\frac{3}{4}$，且 $X_1$ 与 $X_3$ 相互独立。

$(I)$ 求 $Z=X_1 X_3$ 的概率密度 $f_Z(z)$；

$(II)$ 求 $X_1$ 与 $Z$ 的相关系数 $\rho_{X_1 Z}$；

$(III)$ $(X_1+X_2)^2$ 与 $(X_1-X_2)^2$ 是否相互独立？说明理由。

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>见解析</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
(I) 用定义法.
$$\begin{aligned}
F_Z(z) &= P\{Z \leqslant z\} = P\{X_1 X_3 \leqslant z\} \\
&= P\{X_1 X_3 \leqslant z, X_3 = -1\} + P\{X_1 X_3 \leqslant z, X_3 = 1\} \\
&= P\{-X_1 \leqslant z, X_3 = -1\} + P\{X_1 \leqslant z, X_3 = 1\} \\
&= P\{X_3 = -1\}P\{X_1 \geqslant -z\} + P\{X_3 = 1\}P\{X_1 \leqslant z\} \\
&= \frac{1}{4}(1 - P\{X_1 < -z\}) + \frac{3}{4}P\{X_1 \leqslant z\} \\
&= \frac{1}{4}[1 - \Phi(-z)] + \frac{3}{4}\Phi(z) = \frac{1}{4}\Phi(z) + \frac{3}{4}\Phi(z) = \Phi(z),
\end{aligned}$$
其中, $\Phi(z)$ 为 $N(0, 1)$ 的分布函数, 故
$$f_Z(z) = \Phi'(z) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{z^2}{2}}, \quad -\infty < z < +\infty.$$

(Ⅱ) $\operatorname{Cov}(Z, X_1) = \operatorname{Cov}(X_1 X_3, X_1) = E(X_1 X_3 \cdot X_1) - E(X_1 X_3)EX_1$

$$= E(X_1^2 X_3) - (EX_1)^2 EX_3.$$

由 $X_1 \sim N(0, 1)$, 知 $EX_1 = 0, DX_1 = 1$. 又 $X_1^2$ 与 $X_3$ 相互独立, 所以

$$E(X_1^2 X_3) = E(X_1^2)EX_3,$$

$$E(X_1^2) = DX_1 + (EX_1)^2 = 1 + 0^2 = 1,$$

$$EX_3 = (-1) \times \frac{1}{4} + 1 \times \frac{3}{4} = \frac{1}{2},$$

$$\operatorname{Cov}(Z, X_1) = \frac{1}{2}.$$

又 $Z \sim N(0, 1)$, 故 $DZ = 1$, 从而

$$\rho_{X_1 Z} = \frac{\operatorname{Cov}(Z, X_1)}{\sqrt{DZ} \sqrt{DX_1}} = \frac{\frac{1}{2}}{\sqrt{1} \times \sqrt{1}} = \frac{1}{2}.$$

(III) 由 $X_1$ 与 $X_2$ 均服从 $N(0, 1)$, 知 $\overline{X} = \frac{X_1 + X_2}{2}$ 与样本方差 $S^2$ 相互独立, 而

$$S^2 = \left( X_1 - \frac{X_1 + X_2}{2} \right)^2 + \left( X_2 - \frac{X_1 + X_2}{2} \right)^2 = \frac{(X_1 - X_2)^2}{2},$$

所以 $X_1 + X_2$ 与 $(X_1 - X_2)^2$ 相互独立, 故 $(X_1 + X_2)^2$ 与 $(X_1 - X_2)^2$ 相互独立.
</ExamExplanation>

9. 设 2 维随机变量 $(X,Y)$ 的概率密度为
$$f(x,y)=\begin{cases} 2\mathrm{e}^{-(x+y)}, & 0<x<y, \\ 0, & \text{其他.} \end{cases}$$

$(I)$ 求 $(X,Y)$ 的联合分布函数 $F(x,y)$；

$(II)$ 求 $P\left\{X+Y>1\middle|X<\frac{1}{2}\right\}$；

$(III)$ 求 $E(Y-X)$。

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>见解析</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
( I ) $F(x, y) = P\{X \leqslant x, Y \leqslant y\} = \int_{-\infty}^{x} \int_{-\infty}^{y} f(u, v) \mathrm{d}u \mathrm{d}v.$

如图 19-2 所示, 当 $x \leqslant 0$ 或 $y \leqslant 0$ 时, $F(x, y) = 0$.

如图 19-2 所示, 当 $0 < y \leqslant x$ 时,

$$F(x, y) = \int_{0}^{y} \mathrm{d}u \int_{u}^{y} 2e^{-u} e^{-v} \mathrm{d}v = 2 \int_{0}^{y} (e^{-2u} - e^{-y} e^{-u}) \mathrm{d}u$$

$$= \left. (-e^{-2u} + 2e^{-y} e^{-u}) \right|_0^y = 1 - 2e^{-y} + e^{-2y}.$$

如图 19-3 所示, 当 $0 < x < y$ 时,

$$F(x, y) = \int_{0}^{x} \mathrm{d}u \int_{u}^{y} 2e^{-u} e^{-v} \mathrm{d}v = 2 \int_{0}^{x} (e^{-2u} - e^{-y} e^{-u}) \mathrm{d}u$$

$$= \left. (-e^{-2u} + 2e^{-y} e^{-u}) \right|_0^x = 1 - 2e^{-y} - e^{-2x} + 2e^{-(x+y)}.$$

故 $F(x, y) = \begin{cases} 1 - 2e^{-y} - e^{-2x} + 2e^{-(x+y)}, & 0 < x < y, \\ 1 - 2e^{-y} + e^{-2y}, & 0 < y \leqslant x, \\ 0, & x \leqslant 0 \text{ 或 } y \leqslant 0. \end{cases}$

(II) 由 $F_X(x) = \lim_{y \to +\infty} F(x, y) = \begin{cases} 1 - e^{-2x}, & x > 0, \\ 0, & x \leqslant 0, \end{cases}$ 知 $X$ 的边缘概率密度为

$$f_X(x) = \frac{\mathrm{d}F_X(x)}{\mathrm{d}x} = \begin{cases} 2e^{-2x}, & x > 0, \\ 0, & x \leqslant 0, \end{cases}$$

$$P\left\{X + Y > 1 \mid X < \frac{1}{2}\right\} = \frac{P\left(X + Y > 1, X < \frac{1}{2}\right)}{P\left(X < \frac{1}{2}\right)}.$$

又

$$P\left\{X < \frac{1}{2}\right\} = \int_{0}^{\frac{1}{2}} 2e^{-2x} \mathrm{d}x = 1 - e^{-1},$$

$$P\left\{X + Y > 1, X < \frac{1}{2}\right\} = \iint_{\substack{x+y>1 \\ x<\frac{1}{2}}} f(x, y) \mathrm{d}x \mathrm{d}y = \int_{0}^{\frac{1}{2}} \mathrm{d}x \int_{1-x}^{+\infty} 2e^{-(x+y)} \mathrm{d}y$$

$$= 2 \int_{0}^{\frac{1}{2}} e^{-1} \mathrm{d}x = e^{-1},$$

故 $P\left\{X+Y>1\left|X<\frac{1}{2}\right.\right\}=\frac{\mathrm{e}^{-1}}{1-\mathrm{e}^{-1}}=\frac{1}{\mathrm{e}-1}$ 。

（III）记 $Z=Y-X$, 则 $Z$ 的分布函数为

$$
F_{Z}(z)=P\{Y-X \leqslant z\}=\iint_{y-x \leqslant z} f(x, y) \mathrm{dx} \mathrm{dy} .
$$

如图 19-4 所示，当 $z \leqslant 0$ 时, $F_{Z}(z)=0$.
当 $z>0$ 时,

$$
\begin{aligned}
F_{Z}(z) &=\int_{0}^{+\infty} \mathrm{dx} \int_{x}^{x+z} 2 \mathrm{e}^{-x} \mathrm{e}^{-y} \mathrm{dy} \\
&=2 \int_{0}^{+\infty} \mathrm{e}^{-x}\left(\mathrm{e}^{-x}-\mathrm{e}^{-x-z}\right) \mathrm{dx} \\
&=2\left(1-\mathrm{e}^{-z}\right) \int_{0}^{+\infty} \mathrm{e}^{-2x} \mathrm{dx}=1-\mathrm{e}^{-z} .
\end{aligned}
$$

故 $F_{Z}(z)=\begin{cases}1-\mathrm{e}^{-z}, & z>0, \\ 0, & z \leqslant 0,\end{cases}$ 即 $Z=Y-X$ 服从参数 $\lambda=1$ 的指数分布,
从而 $EZ=E(Y-X)=\frac{1}{\lambda}=1$.

1. 设随机变量 $X_1$ 与 $X_2$ 相互独立，且 $X_1 \sim B(1,p)$，$X_2 \sim B(2,p)$，其中 $0<p<1$，令 $Y_1=2X_1+X_2$，$Y_2=X_1-X_2$。

$(I)$ 求相关系数 $\rho_{Y_1 Y_2}$；

$(II)$ 问 $Y_1$ 与 $Y_2$ 是否相互独立？并说明理由。

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>见解析</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
(I) 由 $X_{1}$ 与 $X_{2}$ 相互独立及 $X_{1}\sim B(1,p), X_{2}\sim B(2,p)$, 可知
$\operatorname{Cov}(X_{1},X_{2})=0, DX_{1}=p(1-p), DX_{2}=2p(1-p)$.
由于

$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Cov}(Y_{1},Y_{2}) &=\operatorname{Cov}(2X_{1}+X_{2}, X_{1}-X_{2})=2DX_{1}-DX_{2}-\operatorname{Cov}(X_{1},X_{2}) \\
&=2p(1-p)-2p(1-p)=0,
\end{aligned}
$$

故

$$
\rho_{Y_{1}Y_{2}}=\frac{\operatorname{Cov}(Y_{1},Y_{2})}{\sqrt{DY_{1}}\sqrt{DY_{2}}}=0.
$$

(II) 由于

$$
\{Y_{1}=0\}=\{2X_{1}+X_{2}=0\}=\{X_{1}=0, X_{2}=0\},
$$

$$
\{Y_{2}=0\}=\{X_{1}-X_{2}=0\}=\{X_{1}=0, X_{2}=0\} \cup \{X_{1}=1, X_{2}=1\},
$$

且 $\{Y_{1}=0\} \subset \{Y_{2}=0\}, P\{Y_{2}=0\} < 1$, 故

$$
P\{Y_{1}=0, Y_{2}=0\}=P\{Y_{1}=0\},
$$

$$
P\{Y_{1}=0\} \cdot P\{Y_{2}=0\} < P\{Y_{1}=0\},
$$

从而

$$
P\{Y_{1}=0, Y_{2}=0\} \neq P\{Y_{1}=0\} \cdot P\{Y_{2}=0\},
$$

即 $Y_{1}$ 与 $Y_{2}$ 不相互独立.

**知识点与技巧：** 此题说明两个随机变量不相关是它们相互独立的必要条件而非充分条件.
</ExamExplanation>

2. 设 $X$ 是连续型随机变量，且 $P\{X\leqslant a\}=P\{X>b\}=\frac{1}{4}$，令
$$X_1=\begin{cases} -1, & X\leqslant a, \\ 1, & X>a, \end{cases} \quad X_2=\begin{cases} -1, & X\leqslant b, \\ 1, & X>b. \end{cases}$$
求：$(I)$ $(X_1,X_2)$ 的联合分布及边缘分布；

$(II)$ $\operatorname{Cov}(X_1,X_2), D(X_1-X_2)$。

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>见解析</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
(I) 由

$$
P\{X\leqslant b\}=1-P\{X>b\}=\frac{3}{4}, P\{X\leqslant a\}=\frac{1}{4},
$$

知 $a<b$, 故

$$
P\{X_{1}=-1, X_{2}=-1\}=P\{X\leqslant a, X\leqslant b\}=P\{X\leqslant a\}=\frac{1}{4},
$$

$$
P\{X_{1}=-1, X_{2}=1\}=P\{X\leqslant a, X>b\}=0,
$$

$$
P\{X_{1}=1, X_{2}=-1\}=P\{X>a, X\leqslant b\}=P\{a<X\leqslant b\}=\frac{1}{2},
$$

$$
P\{X_{1}=1, X_{2}=1\}=1-\frac{1}{4}-0-\frac{1}{2}=\frac{1}{4} .
$$

从而 $(X_1, X_2)$ 的联合分布与边缘分布为

| $X_1 \backslash X_2$ | $-1$ | $1$ | $X_1$ 的边缘 |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| $-1$ | $\frac{1}{4}$ | $0$ | $\frac{1}{4}$ |
| $1$ | $\frac{1}{2}$ | $\frac{1}{4}$ | $\frac{3}{4}$ |
| $X_2$ 的边缘 | $\frac{3}{4}$ | $\frac{1}{4}$ | |

$$EX_1 = \frac{1}{2}, E(X_1^2) = 1, EX_2 = -\frac{1}{2}, E(X_2^2) = 1,$$

故 $DX_1 = DX_2 = \frac{3}{4}, E(X_1X_2) = 0$, 从而

$$\operatorname{Cov}(X_1, X_2) = E(X_1X_2) - EX_1 \cdot EX_2 = \frac{1}{4},$$
$$D(X_1 - X_2) = DX_1 - 2\operatorname{Cov}(X_1, X_2) + DX_2 = 1.$$
