<ExamQuestion>

1. 设 $f(x,y) = \arcsin\sqrt{x^2+y^4}$，则下列选项中正确的是（ ）.

<ExamChoices>
<ExamOption>A. $f_x'(0,0)$ 存在，$f_y'(0,0)$ 存在</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>B. $f_x'(0,0)$ 不存在，$f_y'(0,0)$ 存在</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>C. $f_x'(0,0)$ 不存在，$f_y'(0,0)$ 不存在</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>D. $f_x'(0,0)$ 存在，$f_y'(0,0)$ 不存在</ExamOption>
</ExamChoices>

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>B</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
**解析：** $f_x'(0,0) = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x,0) - f(0,0)}{x} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\arcsin|x|}{x} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{|x|}{x}$,
不存在,
$$f_y'(0,0) = \lim_{y \to 0} \frac{f(0,y) - f(0,0)}{y} = \lim_{y \to 0} \frac{\arcsin y^2}{y} = \lim_{y \to 0} \frac{y^2}{y} = 0,$$
故选 B.
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

<ExamQuestion>

2. 设 $f_x'(x_0,y_0)$，$f_y'(x_0,y_0)$ 均存在，则下列选项中正确的是（ ）.

<ExamChoices>
<ExamOption>A. $\lim\limits_{\substack{x \to x_0 \\ y \to y_0}} f(x,y)$ 存在</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>B. $f(x,y)$ 在 $(x_0,y_0)$ 处连续</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>C. $\lim\limits_{x \to x_0} f(x,y_0)$ 存在</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>D. $f(x,y)$ 在 $\mathring{U}(x_0,y_0)$ 内有定义</ExamOption>
</ExamChoices>

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>C</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
**解析：** $f(x,y)$ 在某点偏导数存在不一定在该点连续, 排除选项 B. 也不能推得 $\lim_{\substack{x \to x_0 \\ y \to y_0}} f(x,y)$ 存在, 例如: 设
$$f(x,y) = \begin{cases} \frac{xy}{x^2 + y^2}, & (x,y) \neq (0,0), \\ 0, & (x,y) = (0,0), \end{cases}$$
可知 $f_x'(0,0), f_y'(0,0)$ 都存在, 但 $\lim_{\substack{x \to 0 \\ y \to 0}} f(x,y)$ 不存在, 故排除选项 A.
由 $f_x'(x_0, y_0) = \lim_{x \to x_0} \frac{f(x, y_0) - f(x_0, y_0)}{x - x_0}$ 存在, 只能推得当固定 $y = y_0$ 时, $f(x,y)$ 在 $x_0$ 的邻域内有定义. 而
$$\mathring{U}(x_0, y_0) = \{(x,y) \mid 0 < \sqrt{(x - x_0)^2 + (y - y_0)^2} < \delta\}$$
是圆域, 故选项 D 不正确.
由 $f_x'(x_0, y_0) = \lim_{x \to x_0} \frac{f(x, y_0) - f(x_0, y_0)}{x - x_0}$ 存在, 知 $\lim_{x \to x_0} f(x, y_0) = f(x_0, y_0)$, 故选项 C 正确.
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

<ExamQuestion>

3. 设 $f(x,y) = \begin{cases} \dfrac{x^2+y^2}{x^2+y^4}\sin(xy^2), & x^2+y^2 \neq 0, \\ 0, & x^2+y^2=0, \end{cases}$ 则正确的是（ ）.

<ExamChoices>
<ExamOption>A. $f_{xy}''(0,0)$ 存在，$f_{yx}''(0,0)$ 存在</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>B. $f_{xy}''(0,0)$ 不存在，$f_{yx}''(0,0)$ 存在</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>C. $f_{xy}''(0,0)$ 存在，$f_{yx}''(0,0)$ 不存在</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>D. $f_{xy}''(0,0)$ 不存在，$f_{yx}''(0,0)$ 不存在</ExamOption>
</ExamChoices>

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>B</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
**解析：** 依题设, 有 $f_x'(0,0) = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x,0) - f(0,0)}{x} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{0 - 0}{x} = 0$.
同理, $f_y'(0,0) = 0$.
当 $x^2 + y^2 \neq 0$ 时, 有
$$f_x'(x,y) = \frac{2x(y^4 - y^2)}{(x^2 + y^4)^2} \sin(xy^2) + \frac{(x^2 + y^2)y^2}{x^2 + y^4} \cos(xy^2).$$
故 $f_{xy}''(0,0) = \lim_{y \to 0} \frac{f_x'(0,y) - f_x'(0,0)}{y} = \lim_{y \to 0} \frac{1}{y}$ 不存在.
当 $x^2 + y^2 \neq 0$ 时, 有
$$f_y'(x,y) = \frac{2y(x^2 + y^4) - (x^2 + y^2)4y^3}{(x^2 + y^4)^2} \sin(xy^2) + \frac{(x^2 + y^2)2xy}{x^2 + y^4} \cos(xy^2).$$
故 $f_{yx}''(0,0) = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f_y'(x,0) - f_y'(0,0)}{x} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{0}{x} = 0$. 选项 B 正确.
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

4. 设 $f(x,y) = \sqrt{|xy|}$，则（ ）.

<ExamChoices>
<ExamOption>A. 当 $x>0$ 时，$f_x'(x,y) = -\dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{\left|\dfrac{y}{x}\right|}$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>B. 当 $x<0$ 时，$f_x'(x,y) = \dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{\left|\dfrac{y}{x}\right|}$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>C. 当 $y \neq 0$ 时，$f_x'(0,y) = 0$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>D. 当 $y \neq 0$ 时，$f_x'(0,y)$ 不存在</ExamOption>
</ExamChoices>

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>D</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
**解析：** 对 $y\neq0$，按偏导数定义

$$
f_x'(0,y)=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{\sqrt{|hy|}}{h}.
$$

其绝对值等于 $\sqrt{|y|}/\sqrt{|h|}$，且左右符号不同，因此极限不存在，故 D 正确。

当 $x\neq0$ 时可写成 $f(x,y)=\sqrt{|x|}\sqrt{|y|}$，分别在 $x>0$ 与 $x<0$ 区间求导即可；不能把 $\sqrt{|xy|}$ 误写成 $\sqrt{|x|}\,|y|$。
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

5. 设 $f(x,y) = \begin{cases} \dfrac{2x|y|}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}, & x^2+y^2 \neq 0, \\ 0, & x^2+y^2=0, \end{cases}$ 若 $z = f(\tan t, \ln(1+t))$，则 $\left.\dfrac{\mathrm{d}z}{\mathrm{d}t}\right|_{t=0} = (\quad)$.

<ExamChoices>
<ExamOption>A. $0$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>B. $1$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>C. $2$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>D. $\sqrt{2}$</ExamOption>
</ExamChoices>

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>D</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
**解析：**

$$
\left.\frac{\mathrm{d}z}{\mathrm{d}t}\right|_{t=0}=\lim_{t\to0}\frac{2\tan t\cdot\ln(1+t)}{\sqrt{\tan^2t+[\ln(1+t)]^2}}\cdot\frac{1}{t}=\lim_{t\to0}\frac{2\mid\ln(1+t)\mid}{\sqrt{\tan^2t+[\ln(1+t)]^2}}
$$

$$
=\lim_{t\to0}\frac{2}{\sqrt{\left[\frac{\tan t}{\ln(1+t)}\right]^2+1}}=\frac{2}{\sqrt{2}}=\sqrt{2}.
$$

选项 D 正确.

**注：** $f(x,y)$ 在点 $(0,0)$ 处不可微，以下计算是错误的，由于 $f'_x(0,0)=f'_y(0,0)=0$, 所以

$$
\left.\frac{\mathrm{d}z}{\mathrm{d}t}\right|_{t=0}=\left.\left[f'_x(0,0)(\tan t)'+f'_y(0,0)\frac{1}{1+t}\right]\right|_{t=0}=0+0=0.
$$

</ExamExplanation>

6. 设方程 $xy - z\ln y + \mathrm{e}^{xz} = 1$，存在点 $(0, 1, 1)$ 的一个邻域，在此邻域内该方程（ ）。

<ExamChoices>
<ExamOption>A. 可确定隐函数 $y = y(x, z)$ 和 $z = z(x, y)$ \quad</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>B. 可确定隐函数 $x = x(y, z)$ 和 $z = z(x, y)$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>C. 可确定隐函数 $x = x(y, z)$ 和 $y = y(x, z)$ \quad</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>D. 只能确定隐函数 $z = z(x, y)$</ExamOption>
</ExamChoices>

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>C</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
**解析：** 令 $F(x,y,z)=xy-z\ln y+\mathrm{e}^{xz}-1$, 则 $F(0,1,1)=0$.
$F(x,y,z)$ 对 $x,y,z$ 分别求偏导, 得 $F'_x=y+\mathrm{e}^{xz}\cdot z$, $F'_y=x-\frac{z}{y}$, $F'_z=-\ln y+\mathrm{e}^{xz}\cdot x$,
故
$$F'_x(0,1,1)=2\neq0,\ F'_y(0,1,1)=-1\neq0,\ F'_z(0,1,1)=0,$$
根据隐函数存在定理,知 $F(x,y,z)=0$ 在点 $(0,1,1)$ 的某个邻域内能确定隐函数 $x=x(y,z)$ 和 $y=y(x,z)$,故选 C.
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

8. 设 $f(x, y) = \mathrm{e}^{2x}(x + y^2 + 2y)$，则 $f(x, y)$ 在点 $P\left(\frac{1}{2}, -1\right)$ 处（ ）。

<ExamChoices>
<ExamOption>A. 取得极小值 $-\frac{\mathrm{e}}{2}$ \quad</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>B. 取得极大值 $-\frac{\mathrm{e}}{2}$ \quad</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>C. 取得极大值 $\mathrm{e}$ \quad</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>D. 不取得极值</ExamOption>
</ExamChoices>

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>A</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
**解析：** 由

$$
\begin{cases}
f'_x=\mathrm{e}^{2x}(2x+2y^2+4y+1)=0, \\
f'_y=\mathrm{e}^{2x}(2y+2)=0,
\end{cases}
$$

得驻点 $P\left(\frac{1}{2},-1\right)$. 由于 $A=f''_{xx}(P)=2\mathrm{e}$, $B=f''_{xy}(P)=0$, $C=f''_{yy}(P)=2\mathrm{e}$, 故
$$AC-B^2=2\mathrm{e}\cdot2\mathrm{e}-0>0,\ A=2\mathrm{e}>0,$$
所以 $f\left(\frac{1}{2},-1\right)=-\frac{\mathrm{e}}{2}$ 为极小值,选项 A 正确.
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

10. 设曲面 $S$ 由方程 $F(ax - bz, ay - cz) = 0$ 所确定，$F$ 有连续偏导数，$a, b, c$ 是不为零的常数，则曲面 $S$ 上任一点的切平面都平行于直线（ ）。

<ExamChoices>
<ExamOption>A. $\frac{x}{a} = \frac{y}{b} = \frac{z}{c}$ \quad</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>B. $\frac{x}{b} = \frac{y}{c} = \frac{z}{a}$ \quad</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>C. $\frac{x}{c} = \frac{y}{b} = \frac{z}{a}$ \quad</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>D. $\frac{x}{c} = \frac{y}{a} = \frac{z}{b}$</ExamOption>
</ExamChoices>

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>B</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
**解析：** 设方程 $F(ax-bz, ay-cz)=0$ 确定的隐函数为 $z=z(x, y)$, 则其切平面的法向量 $\boldsymbol{n} = \left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}, \frac{\partial z}{\partial y}, -1\right)$.

方程两边同时对 $x, y$ 求偏导,得

```math
\begin{cases} F_1'\left(a-b\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\right)+F_2'\left(-c\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\right)=0, \\ F_1'\left(-b\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}\right)+F_2'\left(a-c\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}\right)=0. \end{cases}
```

解得

```math
\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = \frac{aF_1'}{bF_1'+cF_2'}, \quad \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = \frac{aF_2'}{bF_1'+cF_2'}
```

设 $\boldsymbol{l} = (b, c, a)$, 由于

```math
\boldsymbol{l} \cdot \boldsymbol{n} = \frac{abF_1'}{bF_1'+cF_2'} + \frac{caF_2'}{bF_1'+cF_2'} - a = \frac{abF_1'+caF_2'-a(bF_1'+cF_2')}{bF_1'+cF_2'} = 0
```

故选 B.
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

<ExamQuestion>

1. $\lim_{\substack{x \to \infty \\ y \to \infty}} \frac{x + y}{x^2 - xy + y^2} = $ _____________.

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>$0$</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
**解析：** 原题为 $x\to\infty,\ y\to\infty$。注意

```math
x^2-xy+y^2=\frac12\bigl[(x-y)^2+x^2+y^2\bigr]\ge \frac12(x^2+y^2)
```

因此

```math
\left|\frac{x+y}{x^2-xy+y^2}\right|
\le \frac{2(|x|+|y|)}{x^2+y^2}
\le \frac{2\sqrt2}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}\to0.
```

故极限为 $0$。
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>$e^{-\frac{1}{2}}$</ExamAnswer>


</ExamExplanation>

3. 设 $z = (1+xy)^y$，则 $\left.\mathrm{d}z\right|_{(1,1)} =$ _________.

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>$dx + (1 + 2\ln 2)dy$</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
**解析：**

```math
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} &= y(1+xy)^{y-1} \cdot y, \\
\frac{\partial z}{\partial y} &= (1+xy)^y \cdot \left[\ln(1+xy) + \frac{xy}{1+xy}\right],
\end{aligned}
```

故

```math
\left.\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\right|_{(1,1)} = 1, \quad \left.\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}\right|_{(1,1)} = 1 + 2\ln 2,
```

于是

```math
\left.dz\right|_{(1,1)} = dx + (1 + 2\ln 2)dy.
```

</ExamExplanation>

4. 设函数 $f(x,y)$ 可微，且 $f(1,2)=2, f_x'(1,2)=3, f_y'(1,2)=4, F(x)=f\bigl(x,f(x,2x)\bigr)$，则 $F'(1) =$ _________.

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>$47$</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
**解析：** 题中

$$F(x)=f\bigl(x,f(x,2x)\bigr).$$

设 $u(x)=f(x,2x)$，则

$$u'(x)=f_x'(x,2x)+2f_y'(x,2x).$$

由 $f(1,2)=2$，在 $x=1$ 时有 $u(1)=2$，于是

$$
F'(1)=f_x'(1,2)+f_y'(1,2)u'(1)
=3+4(3+2\times4)=47.
$$

**技巧：** 多层复合函数先给内层单独命名，再逐层使用链式法则，最不易漏项。
</ExamExplanation>

5. 设 $z = z(x,y)$ 由方程 $e^{2yz} + x + y^2 + z = \frac{7}{4}$ 确定，则 $\left.\mathrm{d}z\right|_{\left(\frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2}\right)} =$ _________.

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>$-\frac{1}{2}(dx + dy)$</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>

```math
\begin{cases}
e^{2yz} \cdot 2y \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} + 1 + \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = 0, \\
e^{2yz} \left(2z + 2y \frac{\partial z}{\partial y}\right) + 2 + \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = 0.
\end{cases}
```

当 $x = y = \frac{1}{2}$ 时, $z = 0$, 代入方程组,解得

```math
\left.\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\right|_{\left(\frac{1}{2}, \frac{1}{2}\right)} = -\frac{1}{2}, \quad \left.\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}\right|_{\left(\frac{1}{2}, \frac{1}{2}\right)} = -\frac{1}{2},
```

故 $\left.\mathrm{d}z\right|_{\left(\frac{1}{2}, \frac{1}{2}\right)} = -\frac{1}{2}(\mathrm{d}x + \mathrm{d}y)$.
</ExamExplanation>

6. 设 $f(x,y) = \int_0^{xy} \frac{\sin t}{1+t^2} \mathrm{d}t$，则 $\left.\frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x^2}\right|_{(0,2)} =$ _________.

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>4</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>


> **注：**这里先将 $y=2$ 代入再对 $x$ 求导。

7. 设 $z(x,y)$ 的全微分 $\mathrm{d}z = (x^2+2xy-y^2)\mathrm{d}x + (x^2-2xy-y^2)\mathrm{d}y$，则 $z(x,y) =$ _________.

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>见解析</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
**解析：** 设第一象限内, 曲线上一任一点为 $P(x, y)$。方程 $3x^2 + 2xy + 3y^2 = a$ 两边同时对 $x$ 求导, 解得

```math
y' = -\frac{3x+y}{x+3y},
```
则过点 $P$ 的切线方程为

```math
Y - y = -\frac{3x+y}{x+3y}(X - x).
```
切线与两个坐标轴的截距分别为

```math
x + \frac{x+3y}{3x+y}y, \quad y + \frac{3x+y}{x+3y}x.
```
三角形的面积为

```math
\begin{aligned}
S &= \frac{1}{2}\left(x + \frac{x+3y}{3x+y}y\right)\left(y + \frac{3x+y}{x+3y}x\right) \\
&= \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{a^2}{a+8xy} \text{ (这里利用了 } 3x^2 + 2xy + 3y^2 = a).
\end{aligned}
```
由已知 $a > 0$, 只需求 $xy$ 在条件 $3x^2 + 2xy + 3y^2 = a$ 下的最大值。
令 $L = xy + \lambda(3x^2 + 2xy + 3y^2 - a)$, 则

```math
\begin{cases}
L'_x = y + 6\lambda x + 2\lambda y = 0, \\
L'_y = x + 2\lambda x + 6\lambda y = 0, \\
L'_\lambda = 3x^2 + 2xy + 3y^2 - a = 0.
\end{cases}
```

解上述方程组,得 $x = y = \frac{\sqrt{2a}}{4}$,故

```math
S_{\min} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{a^2}{a + 8 \cdot \frac{\sqrt{2a}}{4} \cdot \frac{\sqrt{2a}}{4}} = \frac{1}{4},
```
解得 $a = 1$.

```math
& L_y' = 2kx + 2y - 2\lambda y = 0, \\
& L_\lambda' = 1 - x^2 - y^2 = 0
\end{aligned}
\right.
```

得

```math
\left\{
\begin{aligned}
& (1 - \lambda)x + ky = 0, &\text{①} \\
& kx + (1 - \lambda)y = 0, &\text{②} \\
& x^2 + y^2 = 1. &\text{③}
\end{aligned}
\right.
```

① 式和 ② 式构成的方程组有非零解 (因 ① 式和 ② 式构成的方程组的零解不满足 ③ 式), 故

```math

1 - \lambda & k \\
k & 1 - \lambda
\end{vmatrix} = (1 - \lambda)^2 - k^2 = 0,
```

当 $\lambda = 1 - k$ 时, 由 ① 式, 知 $x = -y$, 再联立 ③ 式, 可得

```math
x^2 + y^2 + 2kxy = 1 - 2kx^2 = 1 - k.
```

综上所述, 最大值为 $\lambda_1 = 1 + k$, 最小值 $\lambda_2 = 1 - k$.
故 $\lambda_1 + \lambda_2 = 2$.

```math
\begin{cases}
L_x' = x^{\alpha-1} + \lambda y = 0, \\
L_y' = y^{\beta-1} + \lambda x = 0, \\
L_{\lambda}' = xy - 1 = 0,
\end{cases}
```

解方程组得 $x=y=1$, 由此得到点 $(1,1)$ 是唯一可能的极点. 再应用二元函数极值的充分条件判断, 可知点 $(1,1)$ 是唯一极小值点, 故点 $(1,1)$ 即为最小值点, 最小值 $f_{\min} = f(1,1) = 1$.
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

16. 设 $w = xy - z, z = z(x,y)$ 有二阶连续偏导数，且满足 $\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x^2} + 2\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x \partial y} + \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial y^2} = 0$. 作变换 $u = x + y, v = x - y$.

(I) 求 $\frac{\partial^2 w}{\partial u^2}$;

(II) 若 $\frac{\partial w(0,v)}{\partial u} = ve^{-v}, w(0,v) = \frac{v^2}{4}$，求 $z(x,y)$ 的表达式。

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>见解析</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
**解析：** (Ⅰ) 由 $z = xy - w$, 有

```math
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} &= y - \frac{\partial w}{\partial u} \cdot \frac{\partial u}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial w}{\partial v} \cdot \frac{\partial v}{\partial x} = y - \frac{\partial w}{\partial u} - \frac{\partial w}{\partial v}, \\
\frac{\partial z}{\partial y} &= x - \frac{\partial w}{\partial u} \cdot \frac{\partial u}{\partial y} - \frac{\partial w}{\partial v} \cdot \frac{\partial v}{\partial y} = x - \frac{\partial w}{\partial u} + \frac{\partial w}{\partial v}, \\
\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x^2} &= \frac{\partial^2 w}{\partial u^2} \cdot \frac{\partial u}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial^2 w}{\partial u \partial v} \cdot \frac{\partial v}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial^2 w}{\partial v \partial u} \cdot \frac{\partial u}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial^2 w}{\partial v^2} \cdot \frac{\partial v}{\partial x} = \frac{\partial^2 w}{\partial u^2} - 2\frac{\partial^2 w}{\partial u \partial v} - \frac{\partial^2 w}{\partial v^2}, \\
\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x \partial y} &= 1 - \frac{\partial^2 w}{\partial u^2} \cdot \frac{\partial u}{\partial y} - \frac{\partial^2 w}{\partial u \partial v} \cdot \frac{\partial v}{\partial y} - \frac{\partial^2 w}{\partial v \partial u} \cdot \frac{\partial u}{\partial y} - \frac{\partial^2 w}{\partial v^2} \cdot \frac{\partial v}{\partial y} = 1 - \frac{\partial^2 w}{\partial u^2} + \frac{\partial^2 w}{\partial v^2}, \\
\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial y^2} &= -\frac{\partial^2 w}{\partial u^2} \cdot \frac{\partial u}{\partial y} - \frac{\partial^2 w}{\partial u \partial v} \cdot \frac{\partial v}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial^2 w}{\partial v \partial u} \cdot \frac{\partial u}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial^2 w}{\partial v^2} \cdot \frac{\partial v}{\partial y} = -\frac{\partial^2 w}{\partial u^2} + 2\frac{\partial^2 w}{\partial u \partial v} - \frac{\partial^2 w}{\partial v^2},
\end{aligned}
```

代入原方程式, 得

```math
\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x^2} + 2\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x \partial y} + \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial y^2} = 2 - 4\frac{\partial^2 w}{\partial u^2} = 0,
```

故

```math
\frac{\partial^2 w}{\partial u^2} = \frac{1}{2}.
```

(Ⅱ)

```math
\frac{\partial w}{\partial u} = \int \frac{1}{2} du + \varphi_1(v) = \frac{1}{2}u + \varphi_1(v),
```

由 $\frac{\partial w(0,v)}{\partial u} = ve^{-v}$, 得 $\varphi_1(v) = ve^{-v}$, 故

```math
\frac{\partial w}{\partial u} = \frac{1}{2}u + ve^{-v}, \quad w = \int \left(\frac{1}{2}u + ve^{-v}\right) du + \varphi_2(v) = \frac{u^2}{4} + uve^{-v} + \varphi_2(v),
```

由 $w(0,v) = \frac{v^2}{4}$, 得 $\varphi_2(v) = \frac{v^2}{4}$, 故

```math
w = \frac{u^2}{4} + uve^{-v} + \frac{v^2}{4},
```

从而

```math
\begin{aligned}
z &= xy - w = xy - \left[ \frac{(x+y)^2}{4} + (x+y)(x-y)e^{-(x-y)} + \frac{(x-y)^2}{4} \right] \\
&= xy - \frac{1}{2}(x^2 + y^2) - (x^2 - y^2)e^{-(x-y)}.
\end{aligned}
```

### 选择题

<ExamQuestion>

下列（ ）选项条件成立时，能够推出函数 $f(x,y)$ 在点 $(x_0, y_0)$ 处可微，且全微分 $\left.\mathrm{d}f(x,y)\right|_{(x_0,y_0)} = 0$.

<ExamChoices>
<ExamOption>A. $f'_x(x_0,y_0) = f'_y(x_0,y_0) = 0$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>B. $f(x,y)$ 在点 $(x_0, y_0)$ 处的全增量 $\Delta f = \frac{\Delta x \Delta y}{\sqrt{(\Delta x)^2 + (\Delta y)^2}}$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>C. $f(x,y)$ 在点 $(x_0, y_0)$ 处的全增量 $\Delta f = \frac{\sin[(\Delta x)^2 + (\Delta y)^2]}{\sqrt{(\Delta x)^2 + (\Delta y)^2}}$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>D. $f(x,y)$ 在点 $(x_0, y_0)$ 处的全增量 $\Delta f = [(\Delta x)^2 + (\Delta y)^2] \sin\frac{1}{(\Delta x)^2 + (\Delta y)^2}$</ExamOption>
</ExamChoices>

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>D</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
**解析：** 对于选项 D：当选项 D 中条件成立时，有
$$f_x'(x_0, y_0) = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{\Delta f}{\Delta x} = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \left[ \frac{1}{\Delta x} \cdot (\Delta x)^2 \sin \frac{1}{(\Delta x)^2} \right] = 0,$$
$$f_y'(x_0, y_0) = \lim_{\Delta y \to 0} \frac{\Delta f}{\Delta y} = \lim_{\Delta y \to 0} \left[ \frac{1}{\Delta y} \cdot (\Delta y)^2 \sin \frac{1}{(\Delta y)^2} \right] = 0,$$
$$\lim_{\substack{\Delta x \to 0 \\ \Delta y \to 0}} \frac{\Delta f - df}{\rho} = \lim_{\substack{\Delta x \to 0 \\ \Delta y \to 0}} \frac{\left[ (\Delta x)^2 + (\Delta y)^2 \right] \sin \frac{1}{(\Delta x)^2 + (\Delta y)^2}}{\sqrt{(\Delta x)^2 + (\Delta y)^2}}$$
$$= \lim_{\substack{\Delta x \to 0 \\ \Delta y \to 0}} \left[ (\Delta x)^2 + (\Delta y)^2 \right]^{\frac{1}{2}} \cdot \sin \frac{1}{(\Delta x)^2 + (\Delta y)^2} = 0.$$
由可微的定义，知 $f(x, y)$ 在点 $(x_0, y_0)$ 处可微，且 $df = 0$，选项 D 正确.

对于选项 A：由 $f_x'(x_0, y_0) = f_y'(x_0, y_0) = 0$，知偏导数存在，但不能推出 $f(x, y)$ 在点 $(x_0, y_0)$ 处可微.

对于选项 B：$\Delta f = \frac{\Delta x \Delta y}{\sqrt{(\Delta x)^2 + (\Delta y)^2}}$，当 $\Delta y = 0$ 时，$\Delta f = 0$；当 $\Delta x = 0$ 时，$\Delta f = 0$，故 $f_x'(x_0, y_0) = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{\Delta f}{\Delta x} = 0, f_y'(x_0, y_0) = \lim_{\Delta y \to 0} \frac{\Delta f}{\Delta y} = 0$，由此可知
$$\lim_{\substack{\Delta x \to 0 \\ \Delta y \to 0}} \frac{\Delta f - df}{\rho} = \lim_{\substack{\Delta x \to 0 \\ \Delta y \to 0}} \frac{\frac{\Delta x \Delta y}{\sqrt{(\Delta x)^2 + (\Delta y)^2}}}{\sqrt{(\Delta x)^2 + (\Delta y)^2}} = \lim_{\substack{\Delta x \to 0 \\ \Delta y \to 0}} \frac{\Delta x \Delta y}{(\Delta x)^2 + (\Delta y)^2}$$
不存在，即 $f(x, y)$ 在点 $(x_0, y_0)$ 处不可微.

对于选项 C：由于 $\Delta f = \frac{\sin\left[(\Delta x)^2 + (\Delta y)^2\right]}{\sqrt{(\Delta x)^2 + (\Delta y)^2}}$，故 $f_x'(x_0, y_0) = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{\sin(\Delta x)^2}{|\Delta x| \cdot \Delta x} = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{\Delta x}{|\Delta x|}$，
又因为
$$\lim_{\Delta x \to 0^+} \frac{\Delta x}{|\Delta x|} = 1, \quad \lim_{\Delta x \to 0^-} \frac{\Delta x}{|\Delta x|} = -1,$$
所以 $f_x'(x_0, y_0)$ 不存在. 同理，$f_y'(x_0, y_0)$ 不存在. 故 $f(x, y)$ 在点 $(x_0, y_0)$ 处不可微.
</ExamExplanation>

## 本章知识点与技巧总结

### 1. 多元极限、连续与可微

- 二重极限要对所有趋近路径一致；只验证直线 $y=kx$ 只能用于否定极限，不能单独证明极限存在。
- 偏导数存在不推出连续，更不推出可微；若偏导数在点的某邻域存在并在该点连续，则可推出可微。
- 可微的本质是全增量满足 $\Delta f=f_x\Delta x+f_y\Delta y+o(\rho)$，其中 $\rho=\sqrt{(\Delta x)^2+(\Delta y)^2}$。

### 2. 链式法则与全微分

- 多层复合函数建议先给中间变量命名，再逐层求导，避免漏乘内层导数。
- 对 $z=f(u(x,y),v(x,y))$，有
  $$\mathrm{d}z=f_u\,\mathrm{d}u+f_v\,\mathrm{d}v.$$

### 3. 隐函数求导

- 对 $F(x,y,z)=0$，若 $F_z\neq0$，则
  $$z_x=-\frac{F_x}{F_z},\qquad z_y=-\frac{F_y}{F_z}.$$
- 题目只要求某个导数组合时，不必先完整解出各偏导；常可直接对隐式方程求导后线性组合消元。

### 4. 切平面、法线、方向导数与梯度

- 曲面 $F(x,y,z)=0$ 在点 $P$ 的法向量为 $\nabla F(P)$。
- 方向导数为 $D_{\boldsymbol l}f=\nabla f\cdot\boldsymbol l$（$\boldsymbol l$ 为单位向量）；最大方向导数为 $|\nabla f|$，方向即梯度方向。
- 齐次函数满足 Euler 公式：若 $f(tx,ty)=t^mf(x,y)$，则 $xf_x+yf_y=mf$。

### 5. 无条件极值

- 先解 $f_x=f_y=0$ 找驻点，再用 $A=f_{xx},B=f_{xy},C=f_{yy}$ 判断：
  $$AC-B^2>0,\ A>0\Rightarrow\text{极小};\qquad AC-B^2>0,\ A<0\Rightarrow\text{极大};$$
  $$AC-B^2<0\Rightarrow\text{非极值点}.$$

### 6. 条件极值与最值

- 单约束 $\varphi(x,y)=0$ 用拉格朗日乘数法 $\nabla f=\lambda\nabla\varphi$。
- 闭区域最值必须同时比较：内部驻点、边界上的条件极值、边界端点/特殊点。
- 对二次型约束或距离问题，可优先把目标改成距离平方，避免根号。

### 7. 变量代换与二阶偏微分方程

- 线性变换 $\xi=x+ay,\eta=x+by$ 时，先写一阶微分算子，再连续作用得到二阶偏导，通常比逐项硬算更稳。
- 对 $\partial_x^2+2\partial_x\partial_y+\partial_y^2$，要敏感地识别为 $(\partial_x+\partial_y)^2$。

### 8. 高频易错点

- $f_x,f_y$ 存在时，不能直接写 $\mathrm{d}f=f_x\mathrm{d}x+f_y\mathrm{d}y$；必须先有可微性。
- 求“增加最快方向”时若题目要单位方向，应把梯度归一化；若只要方向向量，梯度本身即可。
- 隐函数求二阶导时，第一次求导所得等式还要继续作全导数，不能把偏导项当常数。
- 从换元变量还原原变量时要同步还原指数、上下限和约束；本章综合题第 14 题就是典型易漏因子的情形。
