1. 设 $D$ 为由直线 $x+y=\frac{1}{2}, x+y=1$ 与两坐标轴所围的区域，

$$
I_1=\iint_D[\ln(x+y)]^9\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y, I_2=\iint_D(x+y)^9\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y, I_3=\iint_D[\sin(x+y)]^9\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y,
$$

则 $(\quad)$。

<ExamChoices>
<ExamOption>A. $I_1\leqslant I_2\leqslant I_3$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>B. $I_1\leqslant I_3\leqslant I_2$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>C. $I_3\leqslant I_2\leqslant I_1$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>D. $I_3\leqslant I_1\leqslant I_2$</ExamOption>
</ExamChoices>

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>B</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
**解析：** $D$ 根据区域条件，在 $D$ 上有
$$
[\ln(x+y)]^9 \leqslant [\sin(x+y)]^9 \leqslant (x+y)^9,
$$
故选 B.
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

2. 设 $D$ 是 $xOy$ 平面上以 $A(1,1), B(-1,1), C(-1,-1)$ 为顶点的三角形区域，$D_1$ 是 $D$ 在第一象限的部分，则 $I=\iint_D(xy+\cos x\sin y)\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y=(\quad)$。

<ExamChoices>
<ExamOption>A. $0$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>B. $2\iint_{D_1}xy\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>C. $2\iint_{D_1}\cos x\sin y\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y$</ExamOption>
<ExamOption>D. $4\iint_{D_1}(xy+\cos x\sin y)\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y$</ExamOption>
</ExamChoices>

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>C</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
**解析：** 三角形区域可写为

$$
D=\{(x,y)\mid -1\le x\le1,\ x\le y\le1\}.
$$

于是

$$
\iint_Dxy\,\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y
=\frac12\int_{-1}^{1}x(1-x^2)\,\mathrm{d}x=0.
$$

再看第二项：

$$
\iint_D\cos x\sin y\,\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y
=\int_{-1}^{1}\cos x\,(\cos x-\cos1)\,\mathrm{d}x.
$$

被积函数关于 $x$ 为偶函数，因此

$$
\iint_D\cos x\sin y\,\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y
=2\int_0^1\cos x\,(\cos x-\cos1)\,\mathrm{d}x
=2\iint_{D_1}\cos x\sin y\,\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y.
$$

故

$$
I=2\iint_{D_1}\cos x\sin y\,\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y,
$$

选 C。
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>$\frac{3}{2}$</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
**解析：** 交换积分顺序：

$$
I=\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\csc 2y\,\mathrm{d}y
\int_0^{\tan y}\frac13x^{-\frac23}\,\mathrm{d}x.
$$

内层积分为 $x^{1/3}$，故

$$
I=\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\frac{\tan^{1/3}y}{\sin2y}\,\mathrm{d}y
=\frac12\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\tan^{-2/3}y\,\mathrm{d}(\tan y).
$$

因此

$$
I=\frac32\tan^{1/3}y\Big|_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}}=\frac32.
$$
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

2. 设 $f(t) = \int_{0}^{t}\mathrm{d}x \int_{x}^{t} \mathrm{e}^{-(x-y)^2}\mathrm{d}y \, (t \geqslant 0)$，则 $f''(1) = $ _______.

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>$e^{-1}$</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
**解析：** 依题设, 有 $\int_0^t \mathrm{d}x \int_x^t e^{-(x-y)^2} \mathrm{d}y$, 知积分区域根据区域条件， 交换积分顺序, 得
$$
f(t) = \int_0^t \mathrm{d}y \int_0^y e^{-(x-y)^2} \mathrm{d}x ,
$$
则
$$
f'(t) = \int_0^t e^{-(x-t)^2} \mathrm{d}x \overset{x-t=u}{=} \int_{-t}^0 e^{-u^2} du = -\int_0^{-t} e^{-u^2} du ,
$$
$$
f''(t) = -e^{-(-t)^2} \cdot (-1) = e^{-t^2} ,
$$
故 $f''(1) = e^{-1}$ .

**注：** $f(t) = \int_0^t \mathrm{d}y \int_0^y e^{-(x-y)^2} \mathrm{d}x = \int_0^t \left[ \int_0^y e^{-(x-y)^2} \mathrm{d}x \right] \mathrm{d}y$, 若记 $g(y) = \int_0^y e^{-(x-y)^2} \mathrm{d}x$, 则 $f(t) = \int_0^t g(y) \, \mathrm{d}y$, 故 $f'(t) = g(t) = \int_0^t e^{-(x-t)^2} \mathrm{d}x$.
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

3. 设 $D: x^2+y^2 \leqslant 4, x \geqslant 0, y \geqslant 0$，$f(x)$ 在 $[0, +\infty)$ 上连续且取正值，则 $I = \iint_{D} \frac{a\sqrt{f(x)} + b\sqrt{f(y)}}{\sqrt{f(x)} + \sqrt{f(y)}}\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y = $ _______.

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>$\frac{a+b}{2}\pi$</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
**解析：** 根据区域条件， $D$ 关于直线 $y = x$ 对称, 故
$$
I = \frac{1}{2} \iint_D \left[ \frac{a\sqrt{f(x)} + b\sqrt{f(y)}}{\sqrt{f(x)} + \sqrt{f(y)}} + \frac{a\sqrt{f(y)} + b\sqrt{f(x)}}{\sqrt{f(y)} + \sqrt{f(x)}} \right] \mathrm{d}x \, \mathrm{d}y
$$
$$
= \frac{a+b}{2} \iint_D \mathrm{d}x \, \mathrm{d}y = \frac{a+b}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{4}\pi \cdot 2^2 = \frac{a+b}{2}\pi .
$$
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

4. 设 $f(x)$ 在 $[0, 1]$ 上连续，且 $\int_{0}^{1} f(x)\mathrm{d}x = A$，则 $I = \int_{0}^{1}\mathrm{d}x \int_{x}^{1} f(x)f(y)\mathrm{d}y = $ _______.

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>$\frac{A^2}{2}$</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
**解析：** 令 $F(x)=\int_{x}^{1}f(y)\mathrm{d}y$，则 $F'(x)=-f(x)$，故
$$
I=\int_{0}^{1}\mathrm{d}x\int_{x}^{1}f(x)f(y)\mathrm{d}y=\int_{0}^{1}f(x)\mathrm{d}x\int_{x}^{1}f(y)\mathrm{d}y
$$
$$
=-\int_{0}^{1}F(x)\mathrm{d}[F(x)]=-\frac{1}{2}F^2(x)\bigg|_{0}^{1}=\frac{A^2}{2}.
$$

**注：** 也可利用交换积分顺序及积分与积分变量无关求解.
$$
\int_{0}^{1}\mathrm{d}x\int_{x}^{1}f(x)f(y)\mathrm{d}y=\int_{0}^{1}\mathrm{d}y\int_{0}^{y}f(x)f(y)\mathrm{d}x=\int_{0}^{1}\mathrm{d}x\int_{x}^{1}f(x)f(y)\mathrm{d}y,
$$
故
$$
2\int_{0}^{1}\mathrm{d}x\int_{x}^{1}f(x)f(y)\mathrm{d}y=\int_{0}^{1}\mathrm{d}x\int_{x}^{1}f(x)f(y)\mathrm{d}y+\int_{0}^{1}\mathrm{d}x\int_{x}^{1}f(x)f(y)\mathrm{d}y
$$
$$
=\int_{0}^{1}\mathrm{d}x\left\{f(x)\left[\int_{0}^{x}f(y)\mathrm{d}y+\int_{x}^{1}f(y)\mathrm{d}y\right]\right\}
$$
$$
=\int_{0}^{1}\mathrm{d}x\int_{0}^{1}f(x)f(y)\mathrm{d}y=\left[\int_{0}^{1}f(x)\mathrm{d}x\right]\left[\int_{0}^{1}f(y)\mathrm{d}y\right]=A^2,
$$
故 $I=\frac{A^2}{2}.$
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

5. 设 $D: x^2+y^2 \leqslant 1, x \geqslant 0, y \geqslant 0$，则 $I = \iint_{D} \frac{1+x-y}{1+x^2+y^2}\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y = $ _______.

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>$\frac{\pi}{4}\ln 2.$</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
**解析：** 根据区域条件， 由于 $D$ 关于直线 $y=x$ 对称, 所以
$$
I=\iint_{D}\frac{1+x-y}{1+x^2+y^2}\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y=\frac{1}{2}\iint_{D}\left(\frac{1+x-y}{1+x^2+y^2}+\frac{1+y-x}{1+y^2+x^2}\right)\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y
$$
$$
=\frac{1}{2}\iint_{D}\frac{2}{1+x^2+y^2}\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y
$$
$$
=\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\mathrm{d}\theta\int_{0}^{1}\frac{r}{1+r^2}\mathrm{d}r=\frac{\pi}{2}\cdot\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\frac{\mathrm{d}(1+r^2)}{1+r^2}
$$
$$
=\frac{\pi}{4}\ln(1+r^2)\bigg|_{0}^{1}=\frac{\pi}{4}\ln 2.
$$
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

$$
I=\iint_{D}\frac{\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}=\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\mathrm{d}\theta\int_{2\cos\theta}^{2\sec\theta}\frac{1}{r}\cdot r\mathrm{d}r

$$
=2\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}(\sec\theta-\cos\theta)\mathrm{d}\theta
$$
$$
=2(\ln|\sec\theta+\tan\theta|-\sin\theta)\bigg|_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}
$$
$$
=2\ln(1+\sqrt{2})-\sqrt{2}.
$$
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

$$
= \frac{1}{2} \left(\frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{9}\right) \int_0^{2\pi} \mathrm{d}\theta \int_0^1 r^3 \, \mathrm{d}r = \frac{13\pi}{144}.

9. 设区域 $D$ 由 $x = -\sqrt{2y-y^2}, x = -2, y = 0, y = 2$ 所围，则 $I = \iint_D y \mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y = \underline{\quad\quad\quad}$．

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>$4 - \frac{\pi}{2}$</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
**解析：** 根据区域条件， 设
$D_{\text{大}} = \{(x, y) \mid -2 \leqslant x \leqslant 0, 0 \leqslant y \leqslant 2\},$
$D_{\text{小}} = \{(x, y) \mid -\sqrt{2y - y^2} \leqslant x \leqslant 0\},$

则
$$
I = \iint_D y \, \mathrm{d}x \, \mathrm{d}y = \iint_{D_{\text{大}}} y \, \mathrm{d}x \, \mathrm{d}y - \iint_{D_{\text{小}}} y \, \mathrm{d}x \, \mathrm{d}y
$$
$$
= \int_{-2}^{0} \mathrm{d}x \int_0^2 y \, \mathrm{d}y - \int_{\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\pi} \mathrm{d}\theta \int_0^{2\sin\theta} r^2 \sin\theta \, \mathrm{d}r
$$
$$
= 4 - \frac{8}{3} \int_{\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\pi} \sin^4\theta \, \mathrm{d}\theta = 4 - \frac{8}{3} \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \cos^4 t \, \mathrm{d}t
$$
$$
= 4 - \frac{8}{3} \times \frac{3}{4} \times \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{\pi}{2} = 4 - \frac{\pi}{2}.
$$

**注：** 由于 $D$ 关于 $y = 1$ 对称, 故
$$
I = \iint_D y \, \mathrm{d}x \, \mathrm{d}y = \iint_D [(y - 1) + 1] \, \mathrm{d}x \, \mathrm{d}y = \iint_D (y - 1) \, \mathrm{d}x \, \mathrm{d}y + \iint_D \mathrm{d}x \, \mathrm{d}y
$$
$$
= 0 + 2^2 - \frac{1}{2} \pi \times 1^2 = 4 - \frac{\pi}{2},
$$
或利用形心纵坐标为 $\bar{y} = 1$, 有 $I = \iint_D y \, \mathrm{d}x \, \mathrm{d}y = \bar{y} \cdot \left(4 - \frac{\pi}{2}\right) = 4 - \frac{\pi}{2}$.
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>

10. 设 $D: x^2+y^2 \leqslant 2x$，则 $I = \iint_D (2x+3y) \mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y = \underline{\quad\quad\quad}$．

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>$2\pi$</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
**解析：** $D$ 根据区域条件， 利用极坐标,
$$
I = \int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \mathrm{d}\theta \int_0^{2\cos\theta} (2r\cos\theta + 3r\sin\theta)r \, \mathrm{d}r
$$
$$
= \int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \left( \frac{16}{3} \cos^4\theta + 8\cos^3\theta \cdot \sin\theta \right) \mathrm{d}\theta

<ExamAnswer>见解析</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
**解析：** 当 $2x - x^2 = (1 - y)^2$ 时, 有

$$

$$

$$



$$
\begin{aligned}
I &= \iint_{D_1} (1 - y)^2 \, \mathrm{d}x \, \mathrm{d}y + \iint_{D_2} (2x - x^2) \, \mathrm{d}x \, \mathrm{d}y \\

\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_0^1 \sqrt{1 - (x - 1)^2} \, \mathrm{d}(x - 1) &\overset{x-1=t}{=} \int_{-1}^0 \sqrt{1 - t^2} \, \mathrm{d}t \overset{t=-u}{=} \int_1^0 \sqrt{1 - u^2} (-\mathrm{d}u) \\

\bar{y} &= \frac{1}{3\pi}\iint_D y\,\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y = \frac{1}{3\pi}\int_0^{2\pi} \mathrm{d}x \int_0^{y(x)} y\,\mathrm{d}y = \frac{1}{6\pi}\int_0^{2\pi} [y(x)]^2\,\mathrm{d}x \\
&= \frac{1}{6\pi}\int_0^{2\pi} (1 - \cos t)^3\,\mathrm{d}t = \frac{5}{6},
\end{aligned}
$$
所以质心为 $\left(\pi, \frac{5}{6}\right)$。
</ExamExplanation>

$$
z = \sqrt{a^2 - x^2 - y^2}.

$$
\begin{cases}
\frac{y}{a} + \frac{z}{-3} = 1, \\
x = 0,
\end{cases}

$$
\begin{cases}
z = \frac{3}{a}y - 3, \\
x = 0,
\end{cases}

&= \frac{\pi}{4} a^2 (a^2 - 3) = 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
解得 $a = \sqrt{3}.$
</ExamExplanation>

26. 设非负函数 $f(x)$ 在区间 $[0, a] (a > 0)$ 上有连续的二阶导数，且 $f(0) = f'(0) = 0$，$f''(x)$ 严格单调递增，平面区域 $D = \{(x, y) \mid 0 \leqslant x \leqslant a, 0 \leqslant y \leqslant f(x)\}$ 的形心坐标为 $(\bar{x}, \bar{y})$。证明：$\bar{x} > \frac{3}{4}a$。

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>见解析</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
**证明：** 由已知,要证
$$
\bar{x} = \frac{\iint_D x \, \mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y}{\iint_D \mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y} = \frac{\int_0^a \mathrm{d}x \int_0^{f(x)} x \, \mathrm{d}y}{\int_0^a \mathrm{d}x \int_0^{f(x)} \mathrm{d}y} = \frac{\int_0^a x f(x) \, \mathrm{d}x}{\int_0^a f(x) \, \mathrm{d}x} > \frac{3}{4}a,
$$
即证 $\int_0^a x f(x) \, \mathrm{d}x > \frac{3}{4}a \int_0^a f(x) \, \mathrm{d}x$。

令 $F(a) = \int_0^a x f(x) \, \mathrm{d}x - \frac{3}{4}a \int_0^a f(x) \, \mathrm{d}x$, 则
$$
F'(a) = \frac{1}{4}af(a) - \frac{3}{4}\int_0^a f(x) \, \mathrm{d}x, \quad F''(a) = -\frac{1}{2}f(a) + \frac{1}{4}af'(a),
$$
$$
F'''(a) = -\frac{1}{4}f'(a) + \frac{1}{4}af''(a) = -\frac{1}{4}[f'(a) - f'(0)] + \frac{1}{4}af''(a)
$$
$$
= -\frac{1}{4}af''(\xi) + \frac{1}{4}af''(a) \quad (0 < \xi < a)
$$
$$
= \frac{1}{4}a[f''(a) - f''(\xi)] .
$$

由 $f''(x)$ 严格单调递增,知 $F'''(a) > 0$, 故 $F''(a)$ 单调递增。
又 $F''(0) = 0$, 故 $F''(a) > F''(0) = 0, F'(a)$ 单调递增。
又 $F'(0) = 0$, 故 $F'(a) > F'(0) = 0, F(a)$ 单调递增。
又 $F(0) = 0$, 故 $F(a) > F(0) = 0$, 所以不等式成立。
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

$(II)$ 求 $f(x)$。

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>见解析</ExamAnswer>

$$
F(x)=\int_0^x f(t)\,\mathrm{d}t.

由于题设关系对任意右端点 $x>0$ 都成立，形心公式给出

$$
\frac{\int_0^x t f(t)\,\mathrm{d}t}{\int_0^x f(t)\,\mathrm{d}t}

$$
\int_0^x t f(t)\,\mathrm{d}t=\frac23xF(x).

xF'(x)=\frac23F(x)+\frac23xF'(x),

故

$$
\boxed{F'(x)=\frac{2F(x)}{x}}.

于是

$$
\frac{F'(x)}{F(x)}=\frac2x,
$$

从而

$$
F(x)=C_1x^2.
$$

因此

$$
\boxed{f(x)=F'(x)=Cx},
$$

其中 $C>0$ 为常数。

2. 设在第一象限内，$x^2 + y^2 = \frac{1}{4}$ 与 $x^2 + y^2 = x^4 + y^4$ 及 $x = 0, y = 0$ 所围区域为 $D$。计算
$I = \iint_{D} \frac{xy}{x^2+y^2}\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y$。

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>见解析</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
**解析：** $D$ 根据区域条件，采用极坐标。
$x^2 + y^2 = \frac{1}{4}$ 的极坐标方程为 $r = \frac{1}{2}$。

$x^2 + y^2 = x^4 + y^4$ 的极坐标方程为 $r = \sqrt{\frac{1}{\sin^4 \theta + \cos^4 \theta}}$。

$$
I = \iint_D \frac{xy}{x^2 + y^2} \mathrm{d}x \mathrm{d}y
$$
$$
= \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \mathrm{d}\theta \int_{\frac{1}{2}}^{\sqrt{\frac{1}{\sin^4 \theta + \cos^4 \theta}}} \frac{r^2 \sin \theta \cos \theta}{r^2} \cdot r \mathrm{d}r
$$
$$
= \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin \theta \cos \theta \cdot \frac{1}{2} r^2 \Big|_{\frac{1}{2}}^{\sqrt{\frac{1}{\sin^4 \theta + \cos^4 \theta}}} \mathrm{d}\theta
$$
$$
= \frac{1}{2} \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\sin \theta \cos \theta}{\sin^4 \theta + \cos^4 \theta} \mathrm{d}\theta - \frac{1}{8} \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin \theta \cos \theta \mathrm{d}\theta,
$$

而
$$
\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin \theta \cos \theta \mathrm{d}\theta = \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin \theta \mathrm{d}(\sin \theta) = \frac{1}{2} \sin^2 \theta \Big|_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} = \frac{1}{2},
$$
$$
\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\sin \theta \cos \theta}{\sin^4 \theta + \cos^4 \theta} \mathrm{d}\theta = \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{2\sin \theta \cos \theta}{2(\sin^4 \theta + \cos^4 \theta)} \mathrm{d}\theta
$$
$$
= \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\sin 2\theta \mathrm{d}\theta}{2(\sin^4 \theta + \cos^4 \theta) + 2\cos^2 \theta \sin^2 \theta - 2\cos^2 \theta \sin^2 \theta}
$$
$$
= \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{-\frac{1}{2}\mathrm{d}(\cos 2\theta)}{(\sin^2 \theta + \cos^2 \theta)^2 + (\cos^2 \theta - \sin^2 \theta)^2}
$$
$$
= -\frac{1}{2} \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\mathrm{d}(\cos 2\theta)}{1 + (\cos 2\theta)^2} = -\frac{1}{2} \arctan(\cos 2\theta) \Big|_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}}
$$
$$
= -\frac{1}{2} [\arctan(-1) - \arctan 1] = -\frac{1}{2} \left(-\frac{\pi}{4} - \frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \frac{\pi}{4},
$$

故 $I = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{\pi}{4} - \frac{1}{8} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{16}(2\pi - 1)$。

**注：** 计算 $\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\sin \theta \cos \theta}{\sin^4 \theta + \cos^4 \theta} \mathrm{d}\theta$ 也可以用以下方法：
$$
\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\sin \theta \cos \theta}{\sin^4 \theta + \cos^4 \theta} \mathrm{d}\theta \underset{\sin \theta = t}{=} \int_0^1 \frac{t \mathrm{d}t}{t^4 + (1-t^2)^2} = \frac{1}{2} \int_0^1 \frac{\mathrm{d}(t^2)}{2t^4 - 2t^2 + 1}
$$
$$
t^2 = u \quad \frac{1}{2} \int_0^1 \frac{\mathrm{d}u}{2u^2 - 2u + 1} = \frac{1}{4} \int_0^1 \frac{\mathrm{d}u}{u^2 - u + \frac{1}{2}}
$$
$$
= \frac{1}{4} \int_0^1 \frac{\mathrm{d}\left(u - \frac{1}{2}\right)}{\left(u - \frac{1}{2}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2} = \frac{1}{4} \times 2 \arctan \frac{u - \frac{1}{2}}{\frac{1}{2}} \Big|_0^1
$$
$$
= \frac{1}{2} \left[\arctan 1 - \arctan(-1)\right] = \frac{\pi}{4}.
$$
</ExamExplanation>

3. 设 $f(x)$ 有连续导数，$f(x)$ 不恒为零，且 $\int_{0}^{1} f(x)\mathrm{d}x = 0$，记 $M = \max_{x \in [0, 1]} |f'(x)|$。

$(I)$ 证明：对 $\forall x \in [0, 1]$，有 $|f(x)| \leqslant \frac{1}{2}M$；

$(II)$ 设 $D = \{(x, y) \mid 0 \leqslant x \leqslant 1, 0 \leqslant y \leqslant x\}$，证明：$\left|\iint_{D} f(y)\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y\right| \leqslant \frac{1}{8}M$。

<ExamSolution>

<ExamAnswer>见解析</ExamAnswer>

<ExamExplanation>
**证明：** (I) 令 $F(x) = \int_0^x f(t)\mathrm{d}t$, 则 $F'(x) = f(x), F''(x) = f'(x), F(0) = 0, F(1) = 0$。
对 $F(x)$ 应用泰勒公式，对于 $\forall a \in [0, 1]$, 有
$$
F(x) = F(a) + F'(a)(x - a) + \frac{F''(\xi)}{2}(x - a)^2, \qquad (1)
$$
其中 $\xi$ 介于 $a$ 与 $x$ 之间。将 $x = 1, x = 0$ 分别代入 (1) 式，有
$$
0 = F(1) = F(a) + F'(a)(1 - a) + \frac{1}{2} F''(\xi_1)(1 - a)^2, \qquad (2)
$$

$$
0 = F(0) = F(a) + F'(a)(0 - a) + \frac{1}{2}F''(\xi_2)(0 - a)^2, \qquad (3)
$$

其中 $\xi_1$ 介于 $1$ 与 $a$ 之间, $\xi_2$ 介于 $0$ 与 $a$ 之间.

(2) $-$ (3), 得
$$
f(a) = F'(a) = -\frac{1}{2}\left[F''(\xi_1)(1 - a)^2 - F''(\xi_2)a^2\right].
$$

故
$$
\begin{aligned}
|f(a)| &= |F'(a)| \leqslant \frac{1}{2}\left[|F''(\xi_1)|(1 - a)^2 + |F''(\xi_2)|a^2\right] \\
&= \frac{1}{2}\left[|f'(\xi_1)|(1 - a)^2 + |f'(\xi_2)|a^2\right].
\end{aligned}
$$

令 $|f'(\xi)| = \max \{ |f'(\xi_1)|, |f'(\xi_2)| \}, \xi \in (0, 1)$, 则
$$
|f(a)| = |F'(a)| \leqslant \frac{1}{2} |f'(\xi)| \left[(1 - a)^2 + a^2\right] \leqslant \frac{1}{2} |f'(\xi)| \leqslant \frac{1}{2}M.
$$

由 $a$ 是 $[0, 1]$ 上任一点, 知 $|f(x)| \leqslant \frac{1}{2}M, x \in [0, 1]$.

(II) 由于
$$
\begin{aligned}
\iint_D f(y) \, \mathrm{d}x \, \mathrm{d}y &= \int_0^1 \mathrm{d}y \int_y^1 f(y) \, \mathrm{d}x = \int_0^1 (1 - y)f(y) \, \mathrm{d}y \\
&= \int_0^1 \left(\frac{1}{2} - y\right)f(y) \, \mathrm{d}y + \frac{1}{2} \int_0^1 f(y) \, \mathrm{d}y \\
&= \int_0^1 \left(\frac{1}{2} - y\right)f(y) \, \mathrm{d}y,
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\left| \iint_D f(y) \, \mathrm{d}x \, \mathrm{d}y \right| \leqslant \frac{M}{2} \int_0^1 \left| \frac{1}{2} - y \right| \mathrm{d}y \overset{t = y - \frac{1}{2}}{=} \frac{M}{2} \int_{-\frac{1}{2}}^{\frac{1}{2}} |t| \, dt = M \int_0^{\frac{1}{2}} t \, dt = \frac{M}{8}.
$$
</ExamExplanation>

</ExamSolution>

</ExamQuestion>
